Street life 3: tip-offs and officers asking around, people asking the player the way, drivers stopping for anyone they hit, parking tickets

AI sets 10-13 (src/game: ai.cpp, ai_game.h, app.cpp, barks.cpp, pedai.cpp, police.cpp, traffic.cpp, traffic_core.cpp):
- Tip-offs:
  - Civilians who see the wanted player note when, where and which way he went.
  - Once the police have lost him a while, one of them may call out to an officer on foot ("Officer! He went that way!") and
    point the way. The police then get the sighting: last-seen position and time, and the heading the units follow.
  - Who and how often: never the timid, each witness once in 2 minutes, 25-45 s between tip-offs.
- Officers asking around: an officer on foot who has lost the suspect for 8 s or more asks someone calm nearby ("Police.
  You see anybody come by in a hurry?"). The person answers or points the way, but only if they saw the player more
  recently than the police did.
- Asked the way:
  - Every 4-8 minutes, someone (more often a tourist) asks the player the way while the player is on a sidewalk, not
    wanted, with nothing in hand.
  - Standing still points down the street (the first time a hint shows) and gets thanks and a wave.
  - Walking off gets "Oh - okay. Thanks anyway."
- Drivers who knock down anyone on foot, not only the player, stop seven times in ten and come to see.
  - A victim who gets up has a word for the driver.
  - If the victim stays down, the driver phones for an ambulance and stays until a medic comes.
- Parking tickets: a beat officer may write up a car the player left in the street for over a minute, with the player
  more than 25 m away and no mission running. Getting back in costs $45 ("Parking ticket -$45").
- Walking round cars: people walking up to someone (the bystander coming to the player knocked flat, helpers, sorry and
  road-rage drivers, officers, delivery drivers, ambulance crews) now step round a parked or stopped car standing across
  the way, taking the shorter way round its corners.
- Smaller changes:
  - Calm people within 38 m turn their heads to follow a passing siren.
  - Onlookers to the player's car crashing have their own lines.
  - Two people walking together get night-out lines between 21:30 and 04:30.
- Fixes:
  - Officers no longer pick someone across the street to stop. This was the cause of an earlier stop test giving up.
  - A bus with an unserved stop ahead stays in its kerb lane instead of leaving it for a turn.
- Tests (app.cpp test code): tipoff, askway, copask, plticket and hitped modes, and a rewritten nearmiss test. The tipoff
  and copask tests keep the player hidden 85-105 m off, so the witnesses aren't recycled.
