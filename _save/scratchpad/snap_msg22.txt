Character shading: skin that scatters light, real eyes behind a cornea, softer hair; the brow ridge shades the eyes

- Skin: pre-integrated subsurface scattering from a falloff table built at start-up (render/skin.cpp), its tint scaled
  by each person's melanin so dark skin keeps a natural terminator; ears, nostrils and fingers transmit light from
  every light, street lamps included; per-region and oiliness roughness, a fine grain and specular anti-aliasing so
  skin no longer turns to plastic at 1-4 m; a faint peach-fuzz sheen; creases occlude warm instead of grey.
- Eyes (the faces batch 1 meshes, now with the shader pupil switched on): the view ray refracts through the cornea
  to the iris plane, so the iris sits behind the cornea with parallax, the limbal ring and fibres; the pupil is drawn
  by the shader and dilates at night; tear-film roughness with specular anti-aliasing; the lids' occlusion shades the
  eye's ambient, reflections and direct light. Distant LODs keep the painted eyes.
- Feature shadows: skin, eyes and teeth within 12 m march 8 steps over 4.5 cm toward the sun and the two strongest
  lamps, so the brow ridge shades the upper lids and the eyes under an overhead light, and eyes and teeth cut off
  light steeper than 40-65 degrees. Together with batch 1's lid meshes this removes most of the "goggles" look at
  night (bright rims round dark eye sockets).
- Hair: depth self-shadowing, pixel-footprint strand coverage with fading tips (no more spiky silhouettes), and no
  grey sky sheen on dark hair.
- Nails are detected by region only.
- --shaderdir (gfx_shaders.cpp): load named .hlsl files from a folder instead of the embedded copies, for shader A/B
  runs without a rebuild (development only).
- Cost at the 196-ped club stop: same draws and descriptors (+1 for the skin table); GPU pass times within machine
  noise across an ABBA pair.
