WHAT (player-facing)
People walking stairs at the pace the AI is about to give them (about 0.5 m/s up, 0.55 m/s down, one tread a step) keep their feet on the treads. Before this, at that pace a sixth of the planted feet stood inside a step (toes in a riser, a foot sunk a whole tread). This covers straight flights, switchbacks, L stairs with landings and curved stairs, plus the step that turns onto a flight from a landing. Walking at today's pace is unchanged or better.

HOW (animator.cpp)
- Below the slow walk (the walk blended with the stand), the swing keeps the walk's clearance in the tests that decide when it touches down, grazes, lets go of its toe-off hold, fades its correction and stays planted. This applies at a steady pace only; setting off and stopping keep their timing.
- Landings are predicted along the turning way (turn rate) and scanned along the landing foot's own axis. They are re-scanned when the landing drifts across or turns from the scanned line. The tread move goes along that line.
- The landing probe sits under the toes. Going up, a foot about to lift probes its landing in its last stance frames.
- Going down, a tread too short for both margins splits the shortfall between heel and ball.
- A swing's tread fit holds only where it comes down near its fitted landing. On stairs, a swing stays above the ground it left through the first part of the swing.
- A swing coming down short of its fit, with its heel well over the nose behind that tread, stands on its forefoot.
