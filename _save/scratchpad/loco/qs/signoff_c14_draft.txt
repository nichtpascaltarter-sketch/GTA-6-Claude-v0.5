WHAT (player-facing)
People walking up a switchback at the stair pace (0.5 m/s) put the first foot after the landing on the first tread of the next flight, not into its riser. People turning onto a flight from a landing (L stairs, switchbacks, spirals going up) no longer change their mind late in a swing about which tread the foot lands on, which used to leave the foot between two treads. The player's walk, run and sprint are unchanged.

HOW (animator.cpp)
- The stairs "seen" window (1.5 s since a step last met a tread) now counts the way walked, not the time: about 1.8 m at any pace. At 0.5 m/s the time window lapsed on a switchback's landing, so the swing toward the second flight never scanned for treads and came down unfitted.
- A swing re-scanned past mid-swing that already has a tread fitted keeps that tread unless another needs a much smaller move (+0.15 m cost to change). A fit that late can't be reached anyway. This applies at a walk and at a steady pace.
  - From 2 to 3 m/s it fades out as the run's landing lead fades in. At a jog or a run it gave mixed results (the player's run up a straight flight went from 0.6% to 2.2% of planted frames lifted); with the fade, those paces behave exactly as in c13.
  - It also gives way while stopping or setting off: without that, a walker stopping mid-flight came down short of the far tread it held and stood 15 cm in the air.

KNOWN LIMITS
- Where the AI holds walkers up on a switchback's landing for a long time, c14 does not help. My first in-game switchback runs had that: the walkers' goals were too near and they stood up to a minute at the turn (st19nh / st20nh). There c14 came out the same as c13 within two footsteps: sunk 3 cm 1.3% -> 1.5%. One of those two was a stance planted with its heel in the tread above while the AI had the body facing back down the flight.
- Tight spirals (walking line under 0.55 m) going down stay at about 24% (own pace); that's the last interior item.
- Unchanged from HEAD: a walker stopping mid-flight going up, or one in a crowd going down, can stand a foot up to 16-17 cm in the air for a few frames (a far tread fit the foot lands short of). That's the next stairs item after the curves.
- The harness spiral with a 0.55 m walking line has an open outer edge at 0.85 m. An outer foot that goes over it shows as a 40-66 cm "slide" and a 0.6-1.2 m "float" in a few runs (c12, c13 and c14 alike). A real spiral has a wall there.
