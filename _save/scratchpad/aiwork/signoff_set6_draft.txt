Car alarms and key fobs:
- A car alarm going off (a knock, or the player breaking in) is heard: people about stop for a look over at it, a few
  grumble ("Somebody turn that thing off!" - more of them late at night), officers on the beat glance over. Seeing the
  player at its door: "Hey! That's not your car!" (ai.cpp car_alarm: STIM_ALARM every second while it goes; pedai.cpp).
- Now and then its owner comes (ai.cpp car_alarm, police_ticket::pickOwner; pedai.cpp ACT_ALARM_OWNER):
  - knocked: over at a jog 4-9 s on, the key fob from a few metres off (the alarm stops with a chirp and a flash of the
    hazards), round to its sidewalk side, crouched for a look at the paint, a word ("Not a scratch. Lucky." - or to the
    player stood by: "Was that you?!"), then in and away half the time, else on foot;
  - broken into: a run to the driver's door shouting ("Hey! That's my car!"); as it goes, stood looking after it with a
    hand to the head ("Come back here!").
- Key fobs: three in four drivers who park lock it a few steps off (a chirp, one flash; traffic.cpp sets VehAI::lockAt),
  owners walking to their car unlock it 6 m off with the arm out (a chirp, two flashes; ACT_DRIVE_OFF).
  SFX_CAR_ALARM_CHIRP existed but was not used anywhere.
- Fixes: a stolen car's alarm never stopped while it was driven (ai.cpp timed out only parked, empty cars) - now
  14-24 s; and a kerbside car (spawned without an AI record) knocked into wailing was never looked at by ai.cpp at all -
  an alarm going now puts the car on record (vehAI), so it times out. Owners only come for ordinary civilian cars.
