AI snapshot-safe, sets 7-9 (on top of sets 5+6). Tested on nt_ai77 = HEAD <hash> plus the blobs below.
[blobs]

Set 7:

- Riding along: a companion whose leader drives off in a car at the kerb (population.cpp departures pick leaders with
  company too) no longer walks on alone: round to a passenger door (pedai.cpp ACT_RIDE_ALONG, a seat each for a group),
  waits there till the driver is in, then in; the car waits for them (VehAI::escortHold, now "anybody on the way to
  it"; traffic.cpp pullOutStep). Riders (PedAI::rider) get out with the driver when the car parks (to the same door),
  and on their own when the driver is gone (the car stood empty at the wheel; or the player at the wheel and slowed:
  out and away with a fleeing line).
- The AI look hook (PedAI::lookAtPt / lookAtT + the extended look block for peds.cpp below - it replaces the in-car-only
  block of sets 5+6): people waiting at the kerb look along the road (the near traffic's way first; at a light only a
  glance each way now and then, at a zebra a proper look) and that way once more as they step off; at a bus stop or
  hailing a taxi, a look down the street the way it will come; a car alarm: the head turned to it (sat ones too); a
  police helicopter low over the streets: a look up at it now and then (ai.cpp AIState::heliAt / heliLow).
- Car alarm owners skip delivery vans whose driver is at a door.
Tests (set 7): rideoff (a walking pair's leader sent to a kerbside car, twice) and crossing (the busiest signalled crosswalk:
each step-off's delay after the light, how many waiting look along the road) modes; census "rides".

Set 8:

- Greetings that were never heard: phone talkers near the player said a line at every free moment (BK_PHONE_CHAT was
  tried every frame), holding the street's one bark slot, so hellos and "are you okay?" were dropped - and a dropped one
  used up the pass. Now a call's line comes once per 5 s per talker (walking or stood), and a greeting drowned out by
  other talk is tried again within the same pass (pedai.cpp).
- Officers busy with somebody (a statement, a sidewalk stop, a ticket) or at a crash scene tell the player standing
  right by them to step back after a few seconds ("Step back, please." - BK_COP_STEP_BACK; ai.cpp; once per 30 s).
- Two waiting side by side at a bus stop a while, within earshot of the player: now and then a word about the bus and
  the answer ("This bus is never on time." / "Tell me about it." - BK_BUS_WAIT / BK_BUS_WAIT_REPLY).
- More lines in the most-heard banks: greetings (any time, morning, evening), bumps, phone calls, honks, filming, dives,
  guns seen, joggers, tourists, music, drunks, crashes, insults, flee, cower, help, thanks (barks.cpp, text only).
Tests (set 8): the walkby mode gives every close pass its word (ai.forceGreet), so the hellos and "are you okay?" are seen; the
pedstop mode puts the player right by the talking officer for 10 s at the first stop; census "step backs".

Set 9:

- The player knocked flat close by (a car, a fall, a blast): people within 14 m who see it gasp ("Oh my God!",
  "Somebody call an ambulance!" - BK_PLAYER_DOWN), the nearest steady one comes over and stands by them, and once
  they are up asks "are you okay?"; the rest stop for a look (pedai.cpp; AIState::playerDownT / playerHelper).
- A driver who knocks the player flat stops seven times in ten (no horn), gets out and runs over: crouched beside them
  while they are down, sorry ("I didn't see you! Are you okay?!" - BK_DRIVER_SORRY), then back to the car once they
  are up and away (ai.cpp detects it, traffic.cpp stops the car, pedai.cpp ACT_ROADRAGE's other way round).
Tests (set 9): hitplayer mode (twice: the player put just ahead of a car at speed); census "sorry drivers", "asked okay".

- A prisoner waiting for the car caught out in the road or on a crossing is walked to the kerb before sitting down (the
  sets 3+4 pedstop test showed them sat on the zebra stripes at a corner; pedai.cpp ACT_CUFFED).
- Mission characters (persistent) are left out of the knocked-down reaction.
