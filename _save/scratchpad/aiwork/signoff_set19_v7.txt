WHAT (player-facing)
- Red lights and stop signs at close pairs of junctions. A car that went through junction A on green used to meet B's red only on the short link between them (6-15 m at 8-15 m/s), so it ran the red or stopped hard on the crosswalk. Now it sees B's light from the lane before A and from inside A's box. A stop sign there, or an amber it can't beat, counts as red.
  - When the link can't hold the car at a red, it waits at A's line, for at most 30 s, so lights that never line up don't trap it.
  - At a stop sign whose line is at A's exit (a link shorter than the car), it stops at the line with its tail still in A's box, and goes in the stop's order from there.
- Left turns on green. A turner creeping toward its hold point in the box was treated as committed as soon as its nose crossed the stop line, so it sped up into the box. The box's own gap check then stopped it in the oncoming lane: a bus was hit at 13 m/s by the car that had started with it. Now it stops at the hold point until the gap check lets it go. If it is over the line when the light goes amber, it still commits and clears the junction.
- Junction standoffs. A permissive left turner past its hold point and the oncoming car it misjudged stopped 2.6-3.3 m apart in the middle of the box and held the junction for good (32 cars waiting a minute and more at node 5040). Inside a box they now count as blocking each other up to 6 m (3 m on lanes), so one backs off.
- Getting round a parked van or truck, a breakdown or an abandoned car. This replaces the old overtake:
  - The car picks the smaller side step that clears the blocker by 0.3 m, within the room the road gives: the same-way lane beside it, the oncoming lane of a two-way road without a median, the shoulder of a one-way road or ramp, or on the kerb side of a town road only its own lane.
  - An in-lane squeeze is allowed close to a junction. Borrowing a lane still needs 30 m before the junction and a clear lane.
  - While passing, the blocker is judged by the car's own body pushed slightly ahead, and the car creeps by at 3 m/s at most. If it stopped too close to steer round, it backs up 3 m first (twice at most). The side step lasts until the blocker is behind it.
  - A lane change stuck behind a parked car is taken over by the pass.
  - Parked cars get a fixed 0.25 m margin (one that grew with speed made a car by a van parked 0.31 m within reach brake and pull away twice a second, until it was taken for stuck).
  - Before: a car stopped a car length behind never got round, because its near corridor stayed where the car was. Near a junction there was no overtake at all. On a one-lane ramp or a one-way street, the old overtake pulled a lane's width over, into the barrier. That fits nt_ai102c's soak, where the player's car sat 150 s on an I-38 on-ramp and wheel-spun into a "show".
- Parked trucks facing you across the street no longer make cars keep to the kerb ("meeting a big vehicle"), which used to press them onto the cars parked on their own side.
- Log only: the traffic health line now names the head of a stuck queue (what holds it) and, when autoplay drives it, the player's own car with its queue. A parked obstacle's position across the path is included.

HOW (all in traffic_core.cpp unless named)
- plan(), stops: stopsAhead() (a stop sign, a red, or an amber that ends before the car gets there) previews the junction after next, from the lane before A (route[2]) and from inside A's box (route[1]). From the lane, at a signal whose link can't hold the car (room < its length + 1 m), it stops at A's line while waitTime < 30 s. From the box, it stops at B's line. At an all-way stop it records preStop (and its arrival) once stopped within 2.2 m of the line, and preGo once stopGrant() and B's exit allow it, or after 25 / 30 s as gate() does. stopGrant()'s queue counts such a car as waiting, and gate() takes stopDone from preStop on B's lane.
- gate(): toHold. A permissive left or U-turn on green with a hold point is no longer committed by crossing the line; it stops at the hold point until the box's gap check lets it go. Amber or red: it commits as before.
- drive(): mutual blocking (nose to nose, the later / higher id backs up after 3 s) counts up to 6 m between cars inside a box, 3 m elsewhere.
- drive(): the pass replaces the overtake. It keeps passHost / passT / passBacks, takes the smaller side step that clears the blocker's extent across the lane by 0.3 m within the road's room (above), and ends when the blocker is behind or drives off. It takes over a stuck lane change, and backs up through the k-turn recovery (straight, 3 m, at most twice).
- plan(), perception: while passing, the blocker is tested against the car's swept box (ahead clamp(0.8 v + 0.5, 0.5, 2.5) m, 0.08 m to the side) instead of the corridor, and the speed is capped at 3 m/s. A still parked body gets a fixed 0.25 m margin. meetBig skips parked bodies and is off while passing.
- ai.cpp aiTrafficHealthText(): the queue head (up to 8 hops), the player's own car when AI-driven, a parked obstacle's offset, "(driving round car N, k back-ups)".
- ai_core.h: Driver gains passHost, passT, passBacks, preStop, preGo.
