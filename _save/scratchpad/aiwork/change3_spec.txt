Change 3's walking-turn model (ai_game.h ai_turn), for the animation agent's harness. Per frame, for a walker asked for velocity
d (|d| 0.35-2.4 m/s, not aiming, not in a fight or a start back from a car), body yaw psi, actual speed v = |vel.xy|:
- turn rate cap: w = lerp(4, 2.4, sat((v - 0.1) / 0.3)) rad/s  (4 stood, 2.4 from 0.4 m/s up); the body turns toward d's
  direction (or the face target) by at most w*dt.
- err = wrap(yaw(d) - psi) (facing the way it goes: |faceYaw - yaw(d)| < 0.6 rad, else - a strafe / step back - the cap only)
- pace: k = lerp(1, 0.08, sat((|err| - 0.87) / 1.4))   (as asked up to 50 deg off, 0.08 of it from 130 deg)
- way: allow = lerp(pi, 0.6, sat((v - 0.15) / 0.35)); the step goes at psi + clamp(err, -allow, allow), speed |d| * k
- movePed then takes the velocity toward that at its 11 / 16 m/s^2 as before.
