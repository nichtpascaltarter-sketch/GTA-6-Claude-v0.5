WHAT (player-facing)
- Red lights at close pairs of junctions. A car that went through junction A on green used to meet B's red only on the short link between them (6-15 m at 8-15 m/s), so it ran the red or stopped hard on the crosswalk. Now it sees B's light from the lane before A and from inside A's box. A stop sign there, or an amber it can't beat, counts as red. When the link can't hold the car, it waits at A's line, for at most 30 s, so lights that never line up don't trap it.
- Left turns on green. A turner creeping toward its hold point in the box was treated as committed as soon as its nose crossed the stop line, so it sped up into the box. The box's own gap check then stopped it in the oncoming lane: a bus was hit at 13 m/s by the car that had started with it. Now it stops at the hold point until the gap check lets it go. If it is over the line when the light goes amber, it still commits and clears the junction.
- Junction standoffs. A permissive left turner past its hold point and the oncoming car it misjudged stopped 2.6-3.3 m apart in the middle of the box and held the junction for good (32 cars waiting a minute and more at node 5040). Inside a box they now count as blocking each other up to 6 m (3 m on lanes), so one backs off.
- Getting round a parked van or truck, a breakdown or an abandoned car. This replaces the old overtake:
  - The car picks the smaller side step that clears the blocker by 0.3 m, within the room the road gives: the same-way lane beside it, the oncoming lane of a two-way road without a median, the shoulder of a one-way road or ramp, or on the kerb side of a town road only its own lane.
  - An in-lane squeeze is allowed close to a junction. Borrowing a lane still needs 30 m before the junction and a clear lane.
  - While passing, the blocker is judged by the car's own body pushed slightly ahead, and the car creeps by at 3 m/s at most. If it stopped too close to steer round, it backs up 3 m first (twice at most). The side step lasts until the blocker is behind it.
  - A lane change stuck behind a parked car is taken over by the pass.
  - Before: a car stopped a car length behind never got round, because its near corridor stayed where the car was. Near a junction there was no overtake at all. On a one-lane ramp or a one-way street, the old overtake pulled a lane's width over, into the barrier. That fits nt_ai102c's soak, where the player's car sat 150 s on an I-38 on-ramp and wheel-spun into a "show".
  - Parked cars get a fixed 0.25 m margin. A margin that grew with speed made a car by a van parked 0.31 m within reach brake and pull away twice a second, until it was taken for stuck.
- Parked trucks facing you across the street no longer make cars keep to the kerb ("meeting a big vehicle"), which used to press them onto the cars parked on their own side.
- Log only: the traffic health line now names the head of a stuck queue (what holds it) and, when autoplay drives it, the player's own car with its queue. A parked obstacle's position across the path is included.
