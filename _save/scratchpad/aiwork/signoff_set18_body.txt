What set 18 does:
- Change 3, walking turns a gait can follow. This is your turn item, with the profile the animation agent ran: "change 3 is fine on the animation side". The code is in ai_game.h, ai_turn.
  - rate(v): the body turns at a rate set by the speed it is actually going. 4 rad/s stood or barely moving (the feet step round), 2.4 rad/s from 0.4 m/s up, so a right angle takes a step or two.
  - follow(): walking, the way the walker goes stays within 35 degrees of the way the body faces from 0.5 m/s up. That makes a turn an arc, not a crab across the old line. Barely moving, any way at all is allowed.
    - The pace eases with the turn's size: as asked up to 50 degrees off, and under a tenth of it by 130 degrees. A turn-round is taken all but on the spot, then off again.
  - ease(): used by every on-foot step.
    - Under 2.4 m/s asked and not aiming, the turn is capped at rate(v). Creeping and standing turns are capped too, at 4 rad/s; they were 6-9.
    - Walking the way it faces, the way and the pace are eased as above.
    - Runs and aiming keep their quick turns. pedai.cpp also leaves out a fight (BRAIN_COMBAT) and a start back from a car.
    - A strafe or a step back, and a quick step out of a runner's way, get the rate cap only.
  - Used by:
    - pedai.cpp's final turn and move. It replaces the 3 rad/s cap on the asked speed and the slower step past 60 degrees. The body turns for the way asked; the step goes the eased way.
    - ai.cpp's BRAIN_FOLLOW and BRAIN_GOTO, which had 8 rad/s and no easing.
    - police.cpp's nine on-foot steps, which had 6-9 rad/s. The tenth, a stand-and-hold, is unchanged.
  - The exact model for the animation agent's harness: rate = lerp(4, 2.4, sat((v-0.1)/0.3)); err = wrap(yaw(asked) - body yaw); pace = lerp(1, 0.08, sat((|err|-0.87)/1.4)); allowed = lerp(pi, 0.6, sat((v-0.15)/0.35)); step direction = body yaw + clamp(err, ±allowed). movePed's accelerations are unchanged.
- Change 2: a BRAIN_GOTO walker behind someone slower on its line keeps their pace a little way back (ai.cpp ai_pace::behind).
  - It counts people 0.35-1.8 m ahead and within 0.6 m of the line.
  - Their pace along the line, a little under it at arm's length, a little over it from 1.55 m.
  - Behind someone standing still it stops, and aiWalkRound's held-up step aside takes it round. Beside the line there is room to pass, so no easing.
  - The sidewalk walkers already did this through pednav's social force.
- The animation agent's follower request: a walking companion's stance no longer flickers (pedai.cpp, with PedAI::followMode/followModeT in ai_game.h).
  - The choice between "stopped with the leader" (facing them, chatting) and "walking along" (facing the way they go, chatting on and off) used to flip frame to frame. The animation agent saw ped 13 flip 27 times in 57 s, and each flip restarted the upper body's crossfade.
  - "Stopped" is entered under 0.25 m/s within 1.1 m, and left over 0.45 m/s or past 1.5 m.
  - "Walking along" is entered above 0.25 m/s asked and left below 0.12.
  - Each choice is kept at least 0.8 s.
- Test modes (app.cpp):
  - gotofollow: a slow BRAIN_GOTO walker 3 m ahead of a quick one on a sidewalk's middle, two rounds. The gap and pace every half second, and any gap under 0.5 m called out.
  - turntest: a BRAIN_GOTO walker's 90 / 135 / 180 degree corners along a sidewalk, at 1.5 and 1.0 m/s. Per corner: the most turn rate over 0.8 m/s, the least speed, the most crab, the time to face the new leg, and the swing off its line. Every 0.1 s for 2.5 s after a corner: the rate, speed and crab.
  - The parking mode keeps its 10:30 only without --time, for the night agent.
