What's in it:
- Police on foot:
  - Sidewalk stops by beat pairs. Each ends one of three ways: let go, a warrant (cuffs, then a transport), or the subject runs (chase and tackle).
  - After an arrest, a witness statement: the officer walks up, asks, takes notes on the device, and the witness points at the scene.
  - Patrol coffee breaks: the car pulls over at the kerb outside a shop, the crew stands by it with coffee, then they drive on.
  - Beat officers' kerbside pauses.
  - A partner brings the car round to an escort walking a prisoner from far off.
  - Dispatch picks units by route length. Spawned units must have a route no longer than 1.7x the straight line, so they no longer drive away first.
  - Held-up units go on foot. Foot chases follow a breadcrumb to the last seen spot.
- Pedestrians:
  - Fleeing bystanders duck into building doors. Cowering ones dash behind a parked car.
  - Walkers sidestep runners. Strangers ask for directions (with a point).
  - Couples walk hand in hand (the data; peds.cpp joins the hands).
- Events: a passer-by gives chase after a purse snatch and sometimes tackles the thief.
- A walk-round for static obstacles (aiWalkRound): benches, planters and bins are avoided by the escort, the prisoner, officers walking back to the car, and every generic GOTO walker.
- Census and test modes: pedstop, copbreak and life; the arrest test window is now 260 s.
