Street life:
- A panhandler (EV_PANHANDLER, events.cpp): someone sitting against a wall with a paper cup. When the player walks by close
  on foot (not wanted, no gun out), he gets up, comes over and asks. Standing still next to him gives $2 (the thanks, a wave);
  walking on gets "no hard feelings". Then he goes back to his spot and sits down; he asks again after the player has been
  30 m away. Now and then a passer-by stops and drops something in his cup, with a word and his thanks.
- Running for the bus (traffic.cpp bus_run, pedai.cpp ACT_ENTER_VEH): when a bus pulls in at a stop, now and then someone
  18-60 m along the sidewalk (in sight of it) waves, calls "Hold the bus!" and runs for the door. Within 16 m the driver
  holds on for them (the timetabled buses already wait for anyone at the door), and they thank the driver at the door.
  If it goes without them, they stand looking after it with a hand to the head and a word ("Seriously?!").
- Startled pedestrians (pedai.cpp): a car tearing past close to the kerb (the player's over 54 km/h, anyone's over
  70 km/h) makes them start back from it and stand looking after it; a word follows ("Slow down!"), more often when it
  was the player.
- Beat officers and jaywalkers (pedai.cpp): an officer who sees someone crossing mid-block close by calls across and points
  ("Crosswalk's right there!"). The jaywalker trots the rest of the way, sometimes with a word back ("Sorry, officer!").
- Personal space (pedai.cpp): the player standing right next to someone sitting or standing about for 3 s gets a look and
  "Can I help you?"; after 9 s they get up and leave the other way ("Weirdo.").
- Being followed (pedai.cpp): the player walking close behind someone for 7 s gets a look back and "Are you following
  me?", and they walk faster. After 15 s, the timid hurry off ("Leave me alone!") and the bold square up ("Keep walking,
  man.").
- Rain: half of those running for it without an umbrella hold a hand over their head (the reach hook).
Traffic:
- Rubbernecking (ai.cpp ai_sights, traffic.cpp, traffic_core.cpp): passing a wreck, a car burning, a police car or
  ambulance stopped with its lights going, someone lying in the street, a fender bender, a breakdown, a fight or a traffic
  stop, three drivers in four slow to 14-25 km/h for a look (on highways, about half the limit). A new host cap in the
  core does this: Driver::hostCap / hostCapT. The old speedCap was reset every plan() and could not be used from outside.
- Cut up by the player (traffic.cpp): a driver who has to stamp on the brakes because the player's car pulled in close
  ahead and much slower gives a long blast of the horn and a shout ("Learn to drive!").
