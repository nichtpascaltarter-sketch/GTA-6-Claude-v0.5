Set 17: the events' people get back into their cars through the door, and the gaps from sets 10-13.

- Event actors get in through the door (ai.cpp ai_board::beginThen). They walk to the door, the door clip runs (startCarEntry), and they are in once it is over.
  - EV_CRASH (a fender bender): after the argument, both drivers walk back to their own cars, get in through the driver's door and drive off into the traffic.
  - EV_BREAKDOWN (a stranded driver): once it is fixed or given up, the driver gets back in through the door and drives off.
  - EV_TRAFFIC_STOP: the officer walks back to the cruiser and gets in through the door. The event lets both cars go once it sees the officer in, as it did before.
  - beginThen(g, id, veh, seat, then): PedAI::boardThen says what happens once they are in.
    - 1: away at the wheel (BRAIN_DRIVER, unparked, engine on, attachTraffic).
    - 2: nothing; the event carries on.
    - begin() is beginThen(..., 0), the set 16 activity follow-ups.
  - Each site falls back to today's warp when the seat has no door, and keeps its old timeout warp (20 or 25 s).
  - Kept instant:
    - EV_TAKEOVER runners (fleeing the police);
    - the arrested crook put into the cruiser (a 25 m put-in that needs the escort system);
    - spawn warps;
    - police bringing the car round to an escort, and the jump back in during a chase;
    - BRAIN_FOLLOW (mission followers), since a follower must not be left behind if the player drives off during the clip.
  - Two LOG lines give the clip lengths: "ai: ped N into car V (seat S) through the door: a X s clip (activity A)" and "ai: ped N in car V (seat S) after its door clip (X s in PS_ENTERING)". A cancel line covers the car going or its seat being taken.
  - events.cpp gets its own forward declaration of ai_board::beginThen.
- The hitped gap (sets 10-13): somebody knocked down by a car whose driver stops to see now waits for the driver.
  - ai.cpp sets PedAI::waitDrv/waitDrvUid at the knock-down. Once up, and not hurt, they stay where they are (pedai.cpp), turned to the driver pulling up, getting out and coming over.
  - They wait for up to 8 s from getting up. They go on 1.5 s after their word ("You could've killed me!"), or as soon as the driver gives it up or drives on.
  - Down hurt is unchanged: the driver phones and stays.
  - The sorry driver's "they're all right, back to the car" clock (3.5 s) now runs only once the driver is with them (within 4 m) or has had the word. Before, it ran while they were still walking over, so a driver who got out 14 m away gave up before reaching them.
  - Test (hitped):
    - The walker is put a quarter second ahead (2-3.2 m), inside the car's stopping distance. On nt_ai83f, a car at 9.3 m/s stopped 2.4 m short of a walker 5 m ahead.
    - The one hit goes back to an ordinary walker from the test's stare. That stare had them walk off toward where the car had been.
    - The status line shows the wait.
- The nearmiss test: the lane pick leaves out highways, ramps and bridges. On nt_ai83 it picked an elevated highway, so the four were stood on its shoulder.
  - When no 150 m run is about, a second round allows 115 m.
  - The four now stand at the kerb 60-105 m ahead.
- Overheard lines from people walking in company (set 10's night chats, and the daytime stroll lines) now come through in a crowd.
  - On nt_ai86's busy night promenade not one was heard in 146 s, with 10-12 walking pairs about. The try came in a single frame every 7 s, and a line is dropped while another voice has just spoken (ai.barkGlobal), so in a crowd nearly every try was lost.
  - Now (pedai.cpp, PedAI::chatT) a pair within 8 m of the player tries every 7 s or so, and one try in three is said. A try lost to another voice goes again a moment later. The rate is otherwise unchanged.
- Walking round a car's end to get to its far side now works off the middle of the car's box (spec.boxCenter), not the body's origin.
  - Affected: the crash drivers going back to their cars (events.cpp EV_CRASH), and aiCarEndToWalkRound's "is that end free" test (ai.cpp).
  - On nt_ai90's crashscene, a long-nosed coupe's front corner, taken off the origin, was inside the car. Its driver pressed against the wing for 25 s until the old warp put him in.
  - Now the corners sit 0.9 m past the box's end and 0.7 m out from its side.
  - The events status line also shows each crash driver's place off their car's box while they walk back.
