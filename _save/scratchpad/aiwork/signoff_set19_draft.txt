WHAT (player-facing)
- Red lights at close pairs of junctions. A car that went through junction A on green used to meet B's red only on the short link between them (6-15 m at 8-15 m/s). It ran the red or stopped on the crosswalk. Now it sees B's light from the lane before A and from inside A's box. If the link can't hold it, it waits at A's line. A stop sign or an amber it can't beat counts as red.
- Junction standoffs. A permissive left turner past its hold point and the oncoming car it misjudged used to stop 2.6-3.3 m apart in the middle of the box and hold the junction for good. Inside a box they now count as blocking each other up to 6 m (3 m on lanes), so one backs off.
- Left turns on green. A turner creeping toward its hold point was treated as committed as soon as its nose crossed the stop line, so it sped up into the box. The box check then stopped it in the oncoming lane, and a bus was hit at 13 m/s. Now it stops at the hold point until the gap check lets it go. Over the line when the light goes amber, it still clears the junction.
- Getting round a parked van or truck, a breakdown or an abandoned car. The fix replaces the old overtake:
  - It picks the smaller side step that clears the blocker by 0.3 m, within the room the road gives: the same-way lane beside it, the oncoming lane of a two-way road without a median, the shoulder of a one-way road or ramp, or on the kerb side its own lane. An in-lane squeeze is allowed close to a junction; a lane borrow still needs 30 m and a clear lane.
  - While passing, the blocker is judged by the car's own body pushed slightly ahead, and the car creeps past at up to 3 m/s. Stopped too close, it backs up 3 m first (at most twice). The side step lasts until the blocker is behind it.
  - A lane change stuck behind a parked car is taken over by the pass.
  - Before: cars stopped a car length behind never got round, because the corridor near the car stayed where the car was. Close to a junction there was no overtake at all. On a one-lane ramp the old overtake pulled a lane's width over, into the barrier.
- Parked trucks facing you across the street no longer make cars keep to the kerb, which used to press them onto the cars parked on their own side.
- Log only: the traffic health line now names what holds the head of a stuck queue and, when autoplay drives it, the player's own car. A parked obstacle's position across the path is included.
