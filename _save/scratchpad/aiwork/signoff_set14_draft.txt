Set 14: the street watches the player's car.

- Heads turn to screeching tyres:
  - ai.cpp lists, every frame, the cars within 80 m of the player whose tyres have screeched for at least 0.25 s: braking hard, sliding or spinning, with wheel slip over 0.55 at over 4 m/s (VehAI::screechT, AIState::screech).
  - Calm people within 30 m turn to look and follow the car with their eyes for 1.6-3 s, using the same follow as a passing siren.
  - Now and then one says something ("Whoa!", "Slow down!" - BK_SCREECH), 7 s apart at most. Census: screech looks.
- The player showing off in one spot (ai.cpp AIState::show*):
  - A show starts after 2 s of the player's car spinning (yaw rate over 1.1 at over 2 m/s) or smoking its rear tyres (under 9 m/s), on the ground, within 20 m of where it began. No show while wanted or on a mission.
  - It ends when the car leaves 40 m, stops for 4 s, or the player gets out.
- Who reacts (pedai.cpp, once per show per person, within 36 m and in sight):
  - Most stop to watch (ACT_WATCH with plShow): turned to the car as it goes round, stepping back out of its path, a cheer or a point every few seconds, and one shout at a time ("Yeah! Send it!" - BK_SHOW_CHEER). The bold mostly film it with the phone up.
  - The steadier sort tut and walk on ("Grow up." - BK_SHOW_TUT); the timid hurry past.
  - When it is over, the crowd breaks up over a few seconds, one in three with a last word ("That was insane!" - BK_SHOW_END).
  - Officers on foot within 45 m call out ("Hey! Knock it off!" - BK_COP_SHOW) and go over for a look. There is no wanted level for it.
  - Census: car shows (watchers, filming, tuts, cop calls).
- The player's car standing over a crosswalk, or up on a sidewalk (pedai.cpp; AIState::plCarOnSidewalk from ai.cpp):
  - People crossing round it (pednav.cpp already walks round a stopped car in the way) glare at the driver and say something: "Hey! I'm walking here!", "Really? On the crosswalk?" (BK_WALKING_HERE).
  - The bold, with their hands free, slap the hood going past: a reach to the bodywork at hood height, and a soft metal thump.
  - On a sidewalk: "Seriously? On the sidewalk?" (BK_SIDEWALK_CAR).
  - Each person reacts at most once every 30 s, by temper: 90% of the bold, 50% of the rest, 20% of the timid.
  - Census: crosswalk words (hood slaps), sidewalk car words.
- The player running past close (pedai.cpp): on foot, not wanted, at over 4.5 m/s within 2.3 m. People turn to look after them, and now and then say something ("Where's the fire?", "Jeez, slow down!" - BK_RUN_PAST; 45% of the bold, 25% of the rest, each person at most once every 20 s). Census: run pasts.
- Late at night (22:00-05:00) on a quiet street (nobody else on foot within 25 m), someone walking alone who meets the player head-on 7-20 m off may be wary: the timid, 45% of women, 12% of the rest.
  - The wary cross over to the other sidewalk if the street allows it (the same check as jaywalking: narrow, nothing coming, no car at the kerb in the way), with a look back.
  - Otherwise they give the player as wide a berth as the sidewalk allows and walk a little faster.
  - Census: night avoids (berths).
  - pedai_detail::overTheStreet() is the jaywalk crossing check, moved into a helper without changes; jaywalking uses it too.
- The player's car stopped close by (under 1.5 m/s, within 9 m) with the radio on (pedai.cpp). Each person reacts at most once every 45 s; the reactions apply to people standing still (waiting at the kerb, or standing about or at a bus stop):
  - The young, the out-on-the-town and the bold may dance to it for 4-8 s where they stand (stance 9 while still): 60% of the lively, 25% of the rest. Now and then one calls out ("Ayy, turn it up!" - BK_GOOD_SONG).
  - The business sort and the timid look over, and 35% say something ("Turn that down!" - BK_LOUD_MUSIC).
  - Census: radio dancers, radio complaints.
- Show watchers are never picked from people out on a crosswalk, so nobody stops in the road to watch.
