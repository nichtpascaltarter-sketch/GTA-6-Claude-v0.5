Fix screenshots and photos that came out as noise on Direct3D 12 (a race in the GPU fence wait)

- Every CPU wait for the GPU shared one auto-reset event. When a wait's 2 s poll timed out just as its fence
  completed, the loop left without consuming the event's signal, and from then on every wait returned one fence
  early, for the rest of the process. CPU readbacks - screenshots, photo-mode photos, the exposure and pixel probes,
  GPU timer reports - then mapped their buffer before the copy had run and wrote out recycled heap memory (the
  "noise" was freed index and vertex buffers). Presented frames were never affected; a resize could also call
  ResizeBuffers with work still in flight. It needs a GPU wait over 2 s, so it struck the software test rig under
  load and would be rare on Windows.
- Fix: the event is reset before SetEventOnCompletion and the wait loops on the fence's completed value, so a
  leftover signal costs one extra pass instead of an early return; a failed wait is fatal instead of a busy spin.
- Self-test: two new cases (16 in all) - a thread signalling the event every millisecond during a long compute job,
  and 24 readbacks with 1 ms polling - fail with the old wait (64/64 and 1152/1536 values wrong) and pass with the fix.
- In game: the same drive with the old logic saved three noise screenshots (the settled re-read of the same buffer
  was correct); with the fix all three shots are clean.
