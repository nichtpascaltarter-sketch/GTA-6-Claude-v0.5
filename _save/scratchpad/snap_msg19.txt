Arrests end in an escort to a patrol car; street brawls; units that slow and get out near a suspect on foot

- Arrests of NPCs: once the cuffs are on, the officer walks the suspect to a patrol car - a step behind and to the
  side, a hand on their arm, at their pace - round the car's end to the back door, and the car drives off with them.
  An officer on a foot beat radios for a transport: the nearest free patrol (or one from out of view, no siren)
  pulls over at the kerb, light bar going, while the suspect sits on the kerb waiting. Onlookers stop to watch and
  film. The suspect walks in a cuffed stance (wrists behind the back, head down).
- Street brawls, a new ambient event: shouting, a friend trying to calm it down, blows, and the police coming for the
  one who started it.
- Police units: within 90 m of a suspect on foot they slow to normal driving and brake for people in their path, and
  the officers get out and go on foot when the car is held up short of the suspect (stopped 3 s or crawling 8 s
  within 75 m). A unit taking a prisoner in is not sent to the next call. Pedestrians wait at the kerb for a siren
  coming through, whatever the crossing signal says.
- Street takeover: the donut car backs off when it gets stuck.
- Tests: new --autoplay brawl; --autoplay arrest and events (with forced-event isolation, so a random ambient chase
  no longer spoils a run); search reworked; takeover logs the nearest police car. The --autoplay cardoor scenario for
  the car-door clips.
