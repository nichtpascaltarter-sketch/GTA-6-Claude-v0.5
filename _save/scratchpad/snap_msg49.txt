Standing on stairs: each foot stays on its tread, so stopping on a flight no longer sinks feet into the steps

Animation: standing on stairs, with each foot kept on its tread (src/anim/animator.cpp; src/anim/character.h adds
Animator fields only):
- What was wrong: stopping on a flight was the worst case left on stairs. The feet were stepped to the flat idle stance
  across the risers, and the stair state lapsed after 1.5 s of standing.
- Every step on stairs now scans the ground along the foot where it lands, whether a quick step, a standing step or a
  foot coming down while standing. Its landing is fitted onto one tread: toes off the riser ahead, heel off the riser
  behind.
- The stair state is kept while standing. A standing step whose fitted landing is where the foot already stands is
  skipped.
- A held foot whose probe sits at the game's 0.45 m clamp no longer feeds the root's vertical move. It under-read it,
  and the stored heights drifted up to 6 cm.
- Checks (animation agent):
  - in game, locostairs with walkers stopping on the flights:
    - sunk 3 cm up 23.9 -> 3.9% and down 17.2 -> 2.6%;
    - slide p90 up 31.9 -> 3.6 cm;
    - slides over 15 cm 38 -> 8.
  - Walking runs are unchanged: identical slides, the same trips and landing errors.
  - harness, stops, 8 seeds: sunk 3 cm up 32.8 -> 1.9% (30 Hz) and 32.6 -> 1.0% (60 Hz).
  - Worse:
    - up scuffs, 25 -> 44 in game: 1-3 frame touches that replace long sinks (frames more than 10 cm into a tread fall
      1249 -> 206);
    - a few more float frames when a walk restarts at 60 Hz.
  - anim_test: ALL PASSED.
