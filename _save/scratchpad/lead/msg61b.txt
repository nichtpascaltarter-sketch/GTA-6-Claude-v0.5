worldv checks stairs with relative ends across a plan's floors

The Codex review of PR #54 found the gap: step 4.1 made relative stair ends part of the format (in a layout that
serves several floors, from=0:<room> and to=+1:<room> / -1:<room>), but the plan pass split floor references only on
a dot, so it skipped every stair.floors check on such a stair without a word.

- src/world/validate_layout.cpp (the plan pass):
  - each end is resolved against the floor being checked. `f0.hall` is unchanged; `0:` is that floor; `+1:` / `-1:`
    is the next floor up / down by height, not by plan order.
  - +1 on a plan's top floor, or -1 on its bottom one, is a stair.floors error naming the floor and the plan.
  - two relative ends that don't climb give one finding for every floor that uses the layout.
  - a relative end's room is checked on the floor it reaches.
  - the treads x rise and derived-tread findings name the spacing, so a typical floor used ten times gives one.
- src/world/validate.cpp: a floor reached only by a typical floor's +1 stair no longer gets a false room.reach.
  checkPlanFloors (validate_layout.h) also returns the floors that relative ends reach, and the plan pass runs first.
- tests/world/worldv_test.cpp, with 9 new fixtures in tests/world/fixtures/plans:
  - tower_b: clean, a typical floor shared by f1 and f2, the top floor reached only through it;
  - tower_c: +1 off the top floor;
  - tower_d: a stair that isn't above.
  testRelativeStairs checks the exact messages and five planted errors on a copy.

Host tools only: the game doesn't compile the validator. Checks (tools engineer, and the lead on this tree): CI's six
GCC steps with its flags pass (-Wall -Wextra -Werror, UBSan), and worldv_test is also clean under ASan.
