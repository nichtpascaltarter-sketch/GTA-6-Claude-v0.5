Palms with folded, drooping fronds and ringed trunks; hedges with a leaf canopy; clear storefront glass

Renderer vegetation phase 3 (src/world/propmesh.cpp palm builders, src/shaders/props.hlsl, src/shaders/matgen.hlsl,
src/render/materials.cpp, src/shaders/facade.hlsli):
- Palms:
  - Fronds are V-folded strips that droop and turn towards the tip.
  - Leaf-scar rings are shaded on the bark: close and deep on coconut palms, shallow and wider on royal palms.
  - Coconuts are round instead of boxes.
  - Palm footprint, collision and anchors are unchanged.
- Hedges, garden shrub masses and the other leaf-material world meshes get a three-layer leaf-canopy material instead
  of flat green.
- Storefront glass: shop windows look into the lit shop through glass 60% towards clear, instead of through the
  tower's tinted curtain-wall glass. (The city's interim storefront-glass stopgap in blockstyle.cpp still stacks with
  this until it is removed.)
- materials.cpp: the material generator table grows to 32 slots with a range check. The leaf-canopy generator (id 28)
  overran the old array in a first test build; it was caught before anything was shared.
