Feet no longer slide when people switch between walking, chatting, phoning or smoking, and a re-planted foot keeps its footprint

Animation (src/anim/animator.cpp, src/anim/character.h):
- A stance change between stances whose legs come from the walk or the stand (plain, chatting, on the phone, smoking,
  waiting) now crossfades the upper body only. The full-body crossfade ran after the feet were put on the ground and
  dragged planted feet with the body's move and turn since the snapshot: the biggest slides in the locomotion tour
  (up to 1.28 m) came from companions flipping between "walking along" and "chatting" and from phone calls and
  cigarettes ending. Every other snapshot (weapon switch, action start and end) still crossfades the whole body.
- A foot held since its toe-off that is planted again (walking, or coming to a stand) keeps its footprint instead of
  being re-planted at the shown heel, which jumped by one update of the pose's own motion.
