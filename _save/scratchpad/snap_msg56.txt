Lips are rose instead of coral, and aviation warning lights flash in step with their red lamps

Faces batch 10 (src/anim/face.cpp; tests/anim/anim_test.cpp):
- Lips were the skin's colour made redder, so they kept its yellow and read salmon or coral at conversation distance.
  Now they are a rose that suits each skin tone (rose-pink on fair skin, muted rose-brown on medium and deep skin):
  darker and redder than the skin round them and 5-7 b* less yellow, as measured on real lips.
- Older people's lips fade towards the skin round them, and their vermilion border softens with age.
- The corners of the mouth are a little darker, so they read as the corners of a mouth.
- Everyone's lips change, the protagonists' included. Shapes, lip sync and expressions are unchanged; only vertex
  colours change (identical triangle counts).
- anim_test gains LipColour: on twelve faces, each also built 40 years older, the lower lip must be redder, less
  yellow and (on light and medium skin) darker than the chin, and the older build's lips at least 10% less red.

Aviation beacon sync (city batch 17: src/world/worldtypes.h, sitegeo.cpp, landmarks.cpp, leisure.cpp, airport.cpp,
port.cpp, rural.cpp, buildmesh.cpp; night renderer: src/render/world_render.cpp, renderer.cpp):
- Each aviation light (LightInstance type 4) carries its lamp's blink phase (new u8 phase, sizeof unchanged), and the
  renderer blinks the light on the lamp's own pattern-1 timing, so the light it casts and the red lamp switch together.
- Each structure flashes as one: Solaris One's spire, the port cranes' boom tips and apexes, the radio mast's levels
  and the sugar mill's stacks. The radio mast and the sugar mill move from the slow blink to the standard rate.
- With Reduce Flashing on, the light pulses gently in step with the lamp's slow pulse instead of blinking.
