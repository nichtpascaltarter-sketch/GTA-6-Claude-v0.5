House garages stay on their own lots: no more garages reaching into the neighbour's house, shop or walk-up

City batch 11 (src/world: buildings.h, buildings.cpp, buildmesh.cpp, blockstyle.cpp):
- The problem: the garage wing or carport beside a house (seven in ten houses, every villa) used to reach past the lot
  line, into the neighbour's garage or house, a shop or a walk-up.
  - 15,293 of the 16,262 wings and carports did (94%), 11,543 of them by more than 2 m.
  - In North Porto Sol a garage stood 2.5 m inside the corner walk-up's lot, against its storefront.
- The fix: a house with a garage now sits off the middle of its lot, away from the garage side, and keeps a 1.8 m side
  yard.
  - On a narrow lot the house is up to 40% narrower.
  - Where the garage still can't fit, the house gets a driveway strip (two concrete wheel tracks) instead.
  - Where even that doesn't fit, it has no garage and no car parked beside it.
  - A house never shifts towards a dead end's turning circle.
- Totals: 15,760 garage wings or carports (was 16,262), 726 driveway strips (was 282), 6,607 houses without a garage
  (was 6,549).
- Back garage rows are no deeper than their plot.
- Side effects:
  - Bodega La Luna moves to the next shop east (building 29652 -> 29653, 12.5 m along the same street). It had been
    pushed off its first choice by a neighbour's garage, and the stick-up encounter follows it.
  - Grove Pantry moves 0.97 m with its villa, and Key Coral Villa 0.16 m. The other 26 interiors are unchanged.
- Code: buildings.h adds garageKind(b) (1 wing or carport, 2 driveway strip, 0 none), houseGarage() and garageSide().
