The hand-built world's file format is frozen at v1, and worldv checks the world files

Stage 0 steps 3 and 4 of the hand-built world (docs/HANDBUILT_WORLD.md). The game is unchanged: the procedural world
stays the default, and --world=hand loads the same terrain as before (worldc writes a byte-identical world.bin apart
from the new manifest and the compressed terrain record).

Step 3 (formatter, schema framework, compression, worldc over all of world/):
- src/world/ntw.cpp/.h: comments are kept, and the canonical formatter writes fields in schema order and precision,
  2-space blocks, lines wrapped at 120; numbers in compounds, ranges and sizes are normalised; CRLF and a BOM are
  read as LF.
- src/world/lz.cpp/.h: an LZ77 codec (LZ4-like) with a bounds-checked decoder. src/world/worldbin.*: LZ sections
  (addCompressed, and bytes() that reads raw or LZ); handworld.cpp reads the terrain record through it.
- tools/worldc.cpp: finds every world/**/*.ntw, parses and schema-checks each (errors as file:line), writes a
  manifest section (file, kind, hash of the text as LF without a BOM, so a CRLF checkout compiles the same world.bin),
  and has --check=<file>. tools/ntwfmt.cpp rewrites files canonically (--check reports). terrainbake writes canonical
  text, and world/terrain/terrain.ntw is now in canonical form (grids byte-identical).

Step 4 (the format, frozen at v1):
- docs/NTW_FORMAT.md: the syntax (files, records, values, units and frames, ids and references, canonical form,
  versions, where files live) and every kind's records and fields in canonical order: roads out of draft with the
  AI's records (lanes, moves, stop lines, signal phases, kerbside, stops, lots, spots, zones, anchors), and the stair
  and door fields of the building kit (doc 4.9). From here it changes only as its section 7 allows.
- src/world/ntwschema.cpp/.h: the schema for all 17 kinds, and schemaRefs(), the reference-target table the
  validator resolves against (87 rows).
- tests/world/world_format_test.cpp: 16 samples (one per kind, a stairs/lift/revolving-door layout, the terrain),
  each clean, canonical, stable, and read the same with CRLF and a BOM; kinds == 17; schemaRefs agreeing with the
  schema both ways; the grid codec, parser, formatter, LZ and world.bin tests.

Checks (city agent): world_format_test with CI's GCC flags (UBSan, -Werror) passes (also re-run by the lead on this
tree); clang -Wall -Wextra -Wvla -Wgnu -Wpedantic clean on the test, worldc and ntwfmt; ntwfmt --check clean on the
16 samples, the 9 examples in the two docs and world/; every field table in NTW_FORMAT.md agrees with the schema;
CRLF and BOM copies compile to an identical world.bin; one --world=hand shot (terrain from the exe, 6 sections).

worldv, the world validator (tools engineer; host tools only, nothing in the game build):
- src/world/validate.cpp/.h, validate_layout.cpp/.h and tools/worldv.cpp. Discovery as worldc (every
  world/**/*.ntw). Per file: file.read, file.parse, file.schema, file.canonical. Across the world: id.form
  (NTW_FORMAT section 5 patterns), id.unique, id.path, ref.form, ref.missing and ref.kind, resolved through
  ntwschema's schemaRefs() table (floor elements through the building's plan and layout).
- Layout checks (doc 9.1 and the stair and door limits of 4.9): wall.cross, room.closed, room.overlap, door.wall,
  window.wall, door.rooms, window.room, door.width (error under 0.8 m clear), door.landing (0.9 m of level landing in
  front of a door), corridor.width (1.0 m), stair.run, stair.tread (error under 0.25 m at the walking line),
  stair.riser, stair.landing, stair.walkline (error under 0.5 m), stair.width/radius/busy warnings, and room.reach
  (every room reachable through doors from a way in; hatches and other floors' stairs count in world runs).
- Diagnostics as file:line: severity: message [check.id]; a summary line; exit 1 on errors; --only=<path or id>;
  --incremental with a hash-keyed cache (build/worldv.cache) that re-resolves references across the world; --jobs=N.
- tests/world/worldv_test.cpp with 78 fixtures (the 16 samples, a 30-file world, 30 bad files marked with the
  findings they must produce): patterns, the schema table both ways, planted errors at exact file:line, geometry,
  the cache, --only, 1 vs 4 threads, every check id reported at least once. Two of the city's samples are excerpts
  and give 6 known layout findings until the city completes them; the test expects exactly those.
- Checks (tools agent, re-run by the lead on this tree): worldv_test passes with CI's GCC flags; worldv reports 0
  errors on world/ and the fixture world; clang -Wshadow-all and -Wconversion clean; a synthetic full-size world
  (37,304 files, 800 layouts) checks in 5.2 s, 1.9 s incremental.

CI (.github/workflows/windows-build.yml, GCC job only; timeout 5 -> 10 minutes): four new steps after the bit-scan
regression tests, with the same flags (-Wall -Wextra -Werror, UBSan): the updater logic test, the world format test,
