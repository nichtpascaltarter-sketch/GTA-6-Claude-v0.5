Street life: parking tickets, police at fender benders, help for the hurt, couples hand in hand, proposals, rain

AI sets 3+4 (src/game: ai.cpp, ai_game.h, app.cpp, barks.cpp, events.cpp, pedai.cpp, police.cpp, traffic.cpp; peds.cpp
gets the couple and reach blocks in animatePed):
- Police:
  - Parking tickets: a beat officer goes round to a parked car's windscreen, writes the ticket on the device and puts
    it under the wiper while the partner waits on the sidewalk.
  - Fender benders: four in ten get a unit sent from out of view. It pulls over behind with the light bar on; one
    officer takes the drivers' details, the other waves traffic round, and the driver who was hit photographs the
    damage.
  - Someone down hurt: a nearby beat pair comes over. One kneels with a hand on their shoulder, the other radios for
    EMS.
  - Arrests:
    - The escort waits with the prisoner at the kerb while a crewmate brings the car round.
    - A transport held up nearby pulls over.
    - A hand on the prisoner's head at the car door.
    - Units only go on foot when close.
  - Officers call out cover and flanking moves. Beat pairs chat, and greet the player.
- Pedestrians:
  - A word for the player walking past: a greeting by time of day, "On your left" from joggers, "Are you okay?" when
    the player is hurt, and a reaction to the horn.
  - Pairs walking together are overheard: a line, then the answer. One pair in two is a couple, walking hand in hand,
    and the pass-by lean is off while they hold hands.
  - A mutter at long red lights, and a press of the crossing button on a pole within reach.
  - Getting lost (a look at the map on the phone, then back the way they came), and stopping to tie a shoelace.
  - Rain: people without umbrellas hurry, some jog, and some shelter under bus stops.
  - A proposal on the sidewalk: down on one knee with a ring while passers-by film. Usually a yes (cheers, a hug, off
    hand in hand), now and then a no.
  - Drivers honk at jaywalkers, who hurry on.
- An AI hand-reach hook (PedAI::reachAt/reachT) drives the arm for the ticket under the wiper, the ring, the crossing
  button, the hand on a shoulder, and the hand on a prisoner's head.
