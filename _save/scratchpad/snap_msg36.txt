Faces at night get lamp light bounced up from the ground; brows, lashes and beards read full; no beard-cheek seam

Renderer character-shading backlog, items 1, 2, 3 and 5 (src/shaders/lighting.hlsl, src/shaders/dynamic.hlsl):
1. Night eye sockets: skin, eyes, hair and cloth within 12 m that face away from a street lamp get the lamp's light
   bounced up from the ground (a fifth of its illuminance, weighted towards normals that face down), so the brow
   ridge and upper lids no longer go black under a steep lamp. A lamp's feature shadow also keeps a tenth of its
   light instead of going fully black.
2. Strand cards dither with their own threshold (the pixel's noise offset by the card's seed), so overlapping brow,
   beard and lash cards add up to 1 - prod(1 - coverage) instead of the largest single card's coverage: brows and
   beards read full instead of as a hatch pattern.
3. Lashes, brows and beards get the feature shadows of the skin they grow on. The hair G-buffer extra byte now holds
   a face-hair flag in bit 3; the per-strand random drops to 4 bits and the card depth keeps its 3 bits.
5. The opaque hair shell wraps its diffuse lighting at 0.15 (cards stay at 0.4), so no dark line opens between beard
   and cheek at the shadow edge.

Checks (renderer agent): facecams before and after at 1920x1080 under the street lamp, at night and by day. At 0.5 m
at night the upper lids go from 5.9 to 4.2 stops below the forehead. Shader compile: 0 failed. Verified on main
111efa9's face meshes; faces batch 5's deeper brow and lash depths still fit the 3 depth bits.
