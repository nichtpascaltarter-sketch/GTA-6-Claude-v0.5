    freeSeat minus claimed seats.
  - Every seat choice that leads to a door clip now honours this: officers returning to their car, the escort car, the
    chase jump-in, ride-alongs, taxi fares, EMS crews, cuffed suspects, coffee breaks, and the road-rage, errand and
    drive-off checks.

    other goes round to their own door.

    pressed against its tail.

    still going round.

    Other pull-overs are unchanged.
  - At a drop-off the cab waits until the fare is out of the exit clip, at most 6 s, instead of driving off while they

  band, with a hard top edge under the brows and a dark bridge between the eyes, most of all under street lamps.
