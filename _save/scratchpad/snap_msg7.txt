Braids and locs, injury reactions, real hospitals, landmark map labels, police around traffic jams

- Hair: cornrows, box braids and locs (long locs, short locs and twists) built from ropes, some in braiding hair of
  their own colour (burgundy, honey blonde, copper, jet black). Locs and box braids don't go under a hat; cornrows
  do, with the rows from the temples running back level above the ears.
- Clothing: tank-top and dress straps lie taut over the collarbone and shoulder blade instead of folding into the
  hollows.
- Animation: an aiming side-step jog between 1.8 and 2.6 m/s, and a back-pedalling jog.
- Injuries: a hit flinches the body by bone and direction, layered over whatever the legs are doing, instead of a
  full-body hit clip that stopped the person. Heavy hits (rifle, bat) knock the body off balance and it catches itself
  with quick steps. Pedestrians shot in the leg limp on that leg; badly hurt ones walk hunched with laboured breathing
  and hold the wound with a free hand. A writhing "down hurt" pose and a bracing fall are authored for the next AI
  pass. The player keeps full control (flinch and stagger only).
- Police: a unit held up in traffic out of the player's sight is moved on past the jam; one stuck close to a suspect
  on foot gets out and goes on foot. Drivers at a crossing hold at the line for a siren coming through.
- Interiors: the interior a story scene is staged in stays loaded for the scene, so a cutscene camera that starts far
  away no longer pushes the people in it out through the building's shell.
- Hospitals: the map blips, the paramedic drop-offs and the respawn after being wasted use the hospitals as the world
  built them (no more respawning in the sea off Fort Castell); Calle Luna Medical is Tidewater General Hospital.
- Map: landmarks (Ocean Promenade, Porto Sol University, Santa Marea Cemetery, the churches, the penitentiary, the
  speedway) are labelled in a tier under the districts, shown when zoomed in.
- Missions and tests: the bait-shop market test runs at night with an approach frame, Paper Trail's autotest takes a
  look at the safe marker before walking in, and traffic_sim gets place anchor dumps.
- Test rig: builds and Wine runs wait for room under the container's memory cap (tools/memfree.sh), and at most three
  shared games run at once, so a compile is no longer killed for memory.
