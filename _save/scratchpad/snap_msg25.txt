Faces batch 2: no more "goggles" round the eyes; higher, softer brows; satin lips; no stripes in the hair at the temples

- Eyes: the lids now blend softly into the socket instead of meeting it in a 3-4 mm groove that read as a dark ring
  with pleats streaking out to the temples:
  - the inner corner is filled;
  - the normals round the eyes are relaxed;
  - the lid rows carry the upper lid's crease.
  Dark skin round the eyes stays only under the eye and at the inner corner. The lower waterline is darker (it sits
  in the lashes' shade), men get a lighter lash line, and eye whites are a shade darker on very dark skin. With the
  renderer's feature shadows, eyes sit in the socket's shade at night instead of glowing.
- Brows: about 2.5 mm higher, leaving brow-bone skin above the lid's fold, so brow, socket and lashes no longer merge
  into one dark bar at 4 m. Heads are sparser, tails thinner, and the skin tint under the brow is slightly stronger.
  The brow ridge stands prouder: the cornea now sits on average 6.4 mm behind the brow front (5.3 before).
- Hair: batch 1's dark and pale stripes beside the eyes are gone (worst on curly hair). The soft, skin-tinted hair
  edge is kept to the front hairline; temples and sides keep a solid hair-coloured edge. Beards get a thinner,
  softer rim where they meet the skin.
- Lips are satin instead of lacquered (gloss 0.85 -> 0.65), and men's lips are a shade less red. Ears are less red,
  older skin is a little blotchier, and forehead lines no longer box in the frown lines.
- Dex now has the stubble his description gives him (src/game/protagonists.h); his seeded draw had left him
  clean-shaven.
- Brows build about twice as fast. A full character mesh builds a few percent faster than batch 1.
- Tests:
  - anim_test ALL PASSED. The new FaceShape test checks eye-region normals for folds, the brow's height above the
    lid fold, the cornea's depth behind the brow and a beard shell's thickness at the lip; it fails on batch-1 code.
  - The faces agent's O2 build on 06580c3 plus these files exits 0.
  - Wine D3D12 portrait runs by day and night are clean.
  - Crowd CPU at the 196-ped club stop: 4.22 ms, against a 4.74 ms base.
