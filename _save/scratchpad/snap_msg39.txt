Stairs: feet land on the treads instead of sinking into the steps; a door-entry hook for AI car entries

Animation (src/anim/animator.cpp, src/anim/character.h; src/game/peds.cpp, src/game/gameworld.h):
- Stair gait:
  - When a swinging foot is about to land where the ground steps (stairs, a kerb), the animator asks the game once
    per step for 12 ground heights, 5 cm apart, along the way ahead.
  - It moves the landing, by at most a fifth of a foot length, onto the nearest tread that fits: toes short of the
    riser ahead, heel clear of the riser behind.
  - Going up, a heel hanging over the nose stands and pivots on the forefoot. Going down, the ground is probed under
    the heel until it clears the nose.
  - The swing clears the tread, and the landing time is predicted from the walker's own steps.
- Foot IK reaches up to two risers (0.45 m, was 0.3 m) from the root, so a foot landing on stairs no longer sinks.
- Hand holding gets a hold axis (AnimInput::grabAxis). It is upright by default; the AI's couple block will use it so
  held hands hang with the palms towards each other.
- GameWorld::startCarEntry(ped, veh, seat) places an AI ped at the seat's door and queues the door clip, so the door
  swings and sounds with it. It returns the clip's length, or -1 for a seat without a door. AI car entries use it next.
