### Scorecard 2 — integrated wave 2 (2026-09-30)
| Category | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Map scale and variety | 5/10 | Same 20.5 km map (23 districts, airport, port, keys, wetlands, landmarks) now with 18 enterable interiors, stunt jumps, wildlife habitats and street-level facade detail; procedural massing still repeats block to block and nothing matches GTA 6's hand-built density of unique places. |
| Visual fidelity | 3/10 | SSGI + probe GI, HiZ SSR, froxel fog, soft shadows, weather, see-through glass, night light pollution and lit shop interiors; tour shots still read as a clean mid-2010s look, and they exposed a blown-out awning-light bug. Far from GTA 6's photoreal lighting, materials and characters. |
| World density and life | 4/10 | A first-person walk shows 90 peds / 60 vehicles around the player, with busy sidewalks, walking groups, idle variations, phone calls, conversations with gestures, bird flocks, gators and dolphins, and ambient events; crowds still lack GTA 6's variety of scripted micro-scenes. |
| Vehicles and driving | 4/10 | 45 original models with LODs, full sim (grip, drifts, burnouts, aquaplaning, damage pull, bikes/boats/aircraft), Tide Customs upgrades with turbo audio, see-through glass that shatters; models are procedural and cabins are basic compared with GTA 6. |
| On-foot and combat | 4/10 | Melee with combos, blocks, dodges, lock-on and synced takedowns (height-matched), gunfire knock-downs and limps, cover, traversal, swimming, parachuting, and a new first-person view; no weapon customization or first-person arm animations yet. |
| NPC and police AI | 4/10 | Lane-graph traffic with signals and indicators, sidewalk peds with errands/groups/reactions (flee, cower, film), foot-beat cops, heli searchlight, loudspeaker pull-overs, tackles; still short of GTA 6's reactive crowds and tactical police. |
| Missions and story | 4/10 | 40 missions registered (acts 1-3, heists with loud/quiet approaches, strangers, bail bonds, races with rivals), phone calls/texts, cutscenes with gestures and lip sync; TTS performances and procedural cutscenes can't match GTA 6's mocap storytelling. |
| Audio | 4/10 | 3D mixer, synthesized engines with turbo/blow-off, 9 radio stations with DJs/ads, formant TTS with personas, emotions, channels (phone/radio/PA/megaphone), crowd walla, animal sounds; synthesized music and voices remain well below licensed music and actors. |
| Performance and stability | 4/10 | Renderer estimated ~8 ms at 1440p High on an RTX 4070 (per-pass timings), gameplay CPU ~2-3 ms with 90 peds/60 cars, ~1.5 GB RAM, crash handler with symbolized stacks; not yet verified on real hardware, and automated runs only go through Wine/llvmpipe. |
