Ordinary blocks get district architecture: about 35 building types, carved footprints, back lots filled in

- Building types: ordinary lots now draw from district palettes of about 35 real types instead of a few generic
  boxes (src/world/blockstyle.cpp, massing.cpp):
  - shops and offices: 1920s shop blocks with cornices, Mediterranean-revival shops under tile pent roofs, MiMo
    storefronts with canopy slabs, corner bodegas with murals, colonnaded arcades, 60s office slabs on pilotis, lofts;
  - apartments: brick walk-ups with fire escapes and water tanks, MiMo garden apartments round courts,
    Med-revival apartments with corner towers;
  - Sol Beach: 70s balcony slabs, glass condos, MiMo resort hotels;
  - houses: ranch, bungalow, colonial, split-level and Key West conch houses with walkable porch decks;
  - villas: Mediterranean, colonial and modernist;
  - the Keys: stilt houses with decks, walkable stairs and tin roofs;
  - industry: sawtooth sheds, warehouses, body shops.
- Footprints are carved inside each lot (L, U, courtyard, stepped, split, corner towers, chamfers, rounded ends,
  wings, podium plus slab). Roofs vary: parapet, eave slab, tile hip or pent, terrace, metal gable, sawtooth,
  butterfly. Flat roofs are white, gravel, silver or tar.
- Variety along a street: each street side favours one type and avoids another, neighbours rarely repeat, and
  corner lots get corner types. Colour and wear vary per building, with district colour palettes. Storefront glass
  stays clear.
- Street level (facadedetail.cpp): arcades, arched window heads, streamline eyebrows, MiMo walkways, murals,
  loading doors, canopies, porches, porticos.
- Block middles (cellgen.cpp):
  - about 3,500 back houses, garage rows and sheds;
  - rear parking lots with stalls, wheel stops, a lamp and a dumpster;
  - fenced lawns and paved service yards.
- Back buildings stay out of gameplay:
  - They are appended after every existing building, so no index shifts.
  - They sit 14 m clear of every street.
  - They use the garage style, which AI doors, venues and police skip.
- Towers get district glass (blue, teal, silver, bronze, dark), white ribbon slabs, precast, granite or
  balcony-tower looks, some with special crowns.
- BuildingSet::pavedAt and pavedGrid tell the renderer where ground is paved, for coming ground-cover work:
  parking and service lots, building footprints, garages, driveways and forecourts.
- Repetition (share of neighbours within 150 m that repeat a building's form): 14.4% -> 3.8% overall.
  Key Solano is 21.6% -> 18.0%; its motels come in the next batch.
- Gameplay is unchanged. The restyle runs after interiors are planned, and interior hosts and site buildings keep
  the plain generator. Every building keeps its centre, axes, extents, front and lot, so doors, garages, storefront
  lights and pointInBuilding behave as before. Story places are identical and 57 missions register.
- Checks: story regression 27 of 27. The world check is clean: no lane intrusions, unguarded falls, steps or steep
  grades. Colliders are up 24% (fences, lamps, porch decks, stairs, back buildings). Draws per tour stop are within
  1%. Near meshes are lighter and the far LOD is heavier, though a far cell is still about 1% of a near one.
  Generation takes about 0.2 s longer.
- tools/memfree.sh reserves 3 GB for each still-growing game; under Direct3D 12 they reach 2.9-3.2 GB.
- tools/run.sh and build.sh: games and builds that are not the lead's snapshot checks raise their own
  oom_score_adj to 500. When the memory cap is hit, the kernel then kills one of them first.
