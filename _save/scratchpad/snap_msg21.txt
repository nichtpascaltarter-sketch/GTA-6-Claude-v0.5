Rear doors: get in and out of the back seats through the door; rear passengers clear the wheel wells

- The passenger key picks the nearest free passenger seat; standing by a rear door you get in through it: the door
  swings wide, the right foot goes in facing ahead, you squeeze in sideways under the roof's edge holding the door's
  frame, sit on the front of the bench, bring the left leg in, slide back and pull the door shut. Getting out is the
  reverse: push the door wide, left leg out, squeeze out sideways, stand behind the door, step back and swing it shut.
  NPC rear passengers get out through their door the same way.
- Rear doors open to 80 degrees (from 70). A coupe's +2 seats behind its front doors, and rear doors narrower than
  0.25 m (the Tanuki), keep the plain clips.
- Rear passengers sit up to 7 cm further inboard where the wheel well rises beside them, elbows closer in, so arms and
  hips no longer press into the wheel-house trim.
- Clipping (carview --check, 12 four-door cars, three body sizes; frames with anything more than 2 cm into the car):
  getting in 18.8% -> 6.8%, getting out 24.7% -> 10.0%. The narrowest openings (Minnow, Kumo) are the worst at 15-30%.
- carview: a point in front of a cabin lining no longer counts as inside the hard panel behind it.
