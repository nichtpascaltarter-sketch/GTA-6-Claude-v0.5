First person: fists come up in a boxing guard while fighting (lead left, rear right; higher and tighter when
blocking, knocked aside by a stagger) and stay up a moment after the last exchange. Each strike drives its fist along
its own path, timed by the move's contact frame: jab and cross straight out turning palm-down, the uppercut rising
from below, the hook swinging round from the side, the counter shove with open hands. A knife is held low in view
whenever it is out (slash across, stab forward); a bat rests on the right shoulder with both hands on the handle (a
level swing, an overhead smash). `--autoplay melee --meleeweapon knife|bat` tests them.

Police: K9 units at two stars and up. Once a suspect on foot has been lost for a while, a handler with a German
shepherd follows the scent trail (the player's path since the police last saw them). The dog tracks ahead with its
nose down, barks when it finds the suspect (the police know where they are again), and is released on a suspect who
runs, taking them to the ground. Traffic: a car that cannot get round a corner at full lock, or that ran into a kerb,
pole or car, backs off with the wheel held over.

past the ramp mouth to a turning circle (77th Boulevard). Where only the expressway and its ramps meet, the median
barrier and its yellow lines run straight through (no blunt barrier end). Lot hedges and garden walls leave a dead
end's turning room open. Sign text on the port and airport signs reads the right way round; the taxi rank and a
planter group moved out of the garage stair towers.
