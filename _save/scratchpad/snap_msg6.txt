Braids and locs, side-stepping jog, injury reactions in the animator, real hospitals, landmark map labels

- Hair: cornrows, box braids and locs (long locs, short locs and twists) built from ropes, some in braiding hair of
  their own colour (burgundy, honey blonde, copper, jet black). Locs and box braids don't go under a hat; cornrows
  do.
- Animation: an aiming side-step jog between 1.8 and 2.6 m/s, and a back-pedalling jog. Impacts and injuries in the
  animator (AnimInput): directional flinches by bone layered over any locomotion; heavy hits that knock the body off
  balance and are caught with quick steps (staggerVelocity for the game); a limp per leg; a hunched, guarded walk with
  laboured breathing when badly hurt; a free hand clutching the wound while standing, walking, crouched or lying hurt
  (stance 24, writhing); and a bracing fall (arms out, chin tucked) to hand over to the ragdoll from.
- Hospitals: the map blips, the paramedic drop-offs and the respawn after being wasted use the hospitals as the world
  built them (no more respawning in the sea off Fort Castell); Calle Luna Medical is Tidewater General Hospital.
- Map: landmarks (Ocean Promenade, Porto Sol University, Santa Marea Cemetery, the churches, the penitentiary, the
  speedway) are labelled in a tier under the districts, shown when zoomed in.
- Missions and tests: the bait-shop market test runs at night with an approach frame, Paper Trail's autotest takes a
  look at the safe marker before walking in, and traffic_sim gets place anchor dumps.
