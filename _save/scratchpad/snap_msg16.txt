Get in and out of cars through the door; tall people fit under low roofs

- Front seats: the player and NPCs get in and out through an opening door. In: walk to the door and pull it open by
  the handle (long coupe doors open less on the pull, so the arm never wraps round the edge), step round its edge into
  the doorway (two steps on a long door), duck in, sit, swing the legs in, pull it shut and buckle up. Out: unbuckle,
  push the door open, turn out, stand holding the frame, push it shut. The door swings, and sounds, with the clip. An
  NPC set to flee or fight as it is taken out keeps the quick exit. Rear seats keep the plain clips for now.
- No more lunges: the hips used to jump up to 30 cm in one frame (the walk to a long door's doorway, a tall person's
  seat fit kicking in); now at most 5 cm per frame getting in and 7.5 getting out.
- Headroom: a seated person whose head would be in the roof slumps under it (the pelvis rolls back, the back rounds,
  the head stays nearly level over the seat, the hips move at most 10 cm); cushions under low roofs sit up to 10 cm
  lower (hips kept 14 cm over the floor); sun visors fold flush under the headliner; hats count. At 1.94 m the Harbor
  front goes from 12.5 cm into the roof to 3.1 cm under it, the Kaito and Gatorback fronts from 23-25 cm into it to
  0.8 cm under; at 1.76 m the low sports cars go from 1-13 cm into it to 2-3 cm under.
- Animator groundwork for the police escort (used once the AI set lands): a cuffed stance and a hand that holds on to
  a point outside clips.
- Test rig: tools/memfree.sh also counts the room still owed to running games and unity compiles until they peak.
  The gates used to see only the room free at launch, so a game and a compile could start together, grow, and get
  killed by the container's memory cap (snapshot 15's first melee run).
- Known, not regressions: the Orsa and Arclight door clips still clip, tall passengers in the coupes' +2 rear seats
  reach the rear glass, and 1.84-1.94 m drivers graze the dash ducking in.
