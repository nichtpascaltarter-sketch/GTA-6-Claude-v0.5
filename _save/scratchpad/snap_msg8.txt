Hurt pedestrians and good samaritans, braced falls, coloured mission markers, crowd hands, speedway ground

- Hurt pedestrians: a passer-by knocked down at very low health stays down on their back, writhing and calling for
  help. Up to two people stop, one kneeling beside them and one on the phone to the emergency line; an ambulance
  comes, its medic kneels with them, and once seen to they get up and leave in the ambulance (or, with nobody coming,
  get up on their own after a couple of minutes and limp off). Limping or badly hurt pedestrians move slower (about
  1 m/s, 1.7 m/s fleeing). New autoplay: hurt.
- Braced falls: trips, street shoves, police tackles, the player's kick and melee knock-downs brace for the fall
  first (arms out, chin tucked, tipping into the push) and the ragdoll takes over from that pose; shots, blasts, cars,
  falls and dives away from cars still go over at once. Nothing of the fall (flinch, stagger, brace) carries into the
  get-up.
- Injury animation: holding a wounded thigh bends the body over it, and the balancing arms bend at the elbows as
  they come up.
- Mission markers: the glow holds a fixed, soft brightness on screen whatever the exposure, so markers no longer
  turn into a blown-out white wall at night; mission start markers are see-through too.
- Missions: a cutscene's first frame already looks through its first shot (the camera sat a frame at the last scene's
  far-away position, and streaming dropped the interior the cast was staged in); in Wake the car gets clear of the
  burning diner before the talk.
- Characters: crowd LOD hands become mittens that keep the hand's length and grip, and far beards are painted onto
  the face; a jacket's colour decides its cloth (no more near-black olive denim), and an untucked top over a belt
  follows the hips below it. Locs meander across the scalp and curls stand off afros.
- Places: the speedway's pit and grandstand strips are paved and the infield mown (wild grass grew through them), and
  the campus Spanish moss is thinner and darker.
- Police: the K9 dog close by in sight of a suspect counts as having found them.
- Test rig: the Direct3D 12 port's validation runs get a Wine slot of their own (NT_D3D12_SLOT).
