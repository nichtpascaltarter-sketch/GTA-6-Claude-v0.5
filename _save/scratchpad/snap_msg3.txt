Live cockpit, fishing, airboat tours, cemetery and churchyards, carrying poses, greetings, Act 4 unlock

- Cockpit: the steering wheel is a live part turned by the driver's hands (a leather rim with thumb grips, three
  dished spokes with switch pads and an airbag hub with the maker's roundel; thinner four-spoke truck and bus wheels;
  a chrome six-spoke boat wheel). The hands grip each vehicle's own rim: its centre, column axis and radius go to the
  animator. Tachometer and speedometer needles sweep over the dials of the player's vehicle; the dial faces are
  near-black with a faint backlight, so the lit scale and the orange needles read by day.
- Fishing: cast from piers, docks, seawalls and river banks, or from a boat that has stopped. Charge the cast, strike
  when the float dips, then reel while keeping the line tension out of the red. Sixteen species by habitat and time
  of day, a cooler the Palmera Angler bait shops buy, and a trophy board.
- Sawgrass Airboat Tours: Jonah's three marsh routes (the Loop and Gator Alley by day, Night Eyes after dark), with
  tourists to show the gators to, star ratings and a tip jar.
- Missions: Act 4 unlocks after the ending (a title card, then Lucha's call); the pier bait shop and the airboat tours
  in the economy; encounter fixes (a leftover result banner clears when a new encounter is seen, Scooter Snatch is a
  camera bag throughout); mission tests for the encounters, each fishing habitat, the airboat tours and the Act 4
  unlock.
- World: Santa Marea Cemetery in Calle Luna (a walled city of the dead with wall vaults, a cypress avenue to the
  chapel, family tombs, mausoleums and society tombs) and town churches with churchyards. Gates in the middle of a
  block (cemetery, campus) join the sidewalk network, and the campus lawns and walks get a ground pad at sidewalk
  level.
- Crowds at named places: diners and a waiter on hotel terraces, students on the campus lawn and steps, spectators,
  mourners, runners doing laps and strollers on park walks, filled only near the player. Autoplay "places" visits
  them with a census at each.
- Carrying: the holding arm is posed for its prop. A roller case trails behind the right hip, bags and briefcases
  hang from a straight arm, a cup is held up at the waist, an open umbrella's shaft is held at the chest, and a
  fishing rod is held up and forward. A tote or crossbody bag damps that arm's swing. A phone at the ear or a
  cigarette takes the right hand: a coffee moves to the left hand, and cases, umbrellas, rods and boards are put away
  meanwhile (the animator and the game share Anim::rightHandBusy). CARRY_HANDSFREE marks people whose hands stay empty.
- Animation: paired greetings (an embrace with pats on the back, a handshake, a kiss on the cheek) sized from both
  skeletons; the knee bends about its hinge by exactly the angle that puts the ankle on target, so feet no longer
  slide at the start of weight shifts; animation tests for standing, driving and greetings.
- Characters: skirts ride on the thighs in a stride and lie over the lap when seated; shoes get laces over the tongue
  with eyelets, crossed laces and a bow; a LOD0 triangle budget for heavy outfit combinations and capped hair-card
  density.
