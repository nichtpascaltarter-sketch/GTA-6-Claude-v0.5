Bindless materials and per-draw instance data on Direct3D 12; the SkyLine glows less at night; no more sawtooth C-pillars

- Per-draw data: world cells pass their offset as root constants instead of a 512-byte constant buffer per cell
  (G-buffer, decals, shadow cascades, env probe, overhead map). Dynamic objects (vehicles, characters, props in
  motion) read one per-frame object buffer written once in prepare(), each draw picking its entry with one root
  constant, instead of uploading a 512-byte constant buffer and setting two root CBVs per draw.
- Bindless materials: the material table, the albedo and normal arrays, foliage cards, the facade table, the sign
  atlas and the facade night lights are read through the bindless heap arrays, with their indices in the frame
  constants (materials.hlsli, facade.hlsli); no pass binds t10-t15 any more. Groundwork for per-object textures and
  ExecuteIndirect.
- gfx: root constants record only the DWORDs that change; a descriptor table is kept when the next shader reads a
  subset of its slots; --gfxstats logs per-frame API counters every 30 frames; --gfxselftest has 19 checks (new: per-
  draw root constants selecting instance data, bindless array and structured reads in a pixel shader, a table kept
  across a shader switch).
- Per frame at the tour stops and streets measured: constant-buffer writes 350-660 (100-290 KB) -> 68-72 (25-28 KB),
  root CBV sets 390-800 -> 83-87, descriptors copied 1,200-2,500 -> 920-1,050.
- Images are unchanged: the downtown night street is pixel-identical to the previous build (RMSE 0), and the other
  shots differ only in ped poses, rain ripples and foliage wind.
- SkyLine: the stair-canopy lights and the platform lamp strips are downlights. They were omni lights 0.35 m under
  the panels, which burned the guideway's underside to near white at night.
- Car bodies: the side glass's slanted rear edge kept a W-shaped kink where its two end rows failed a strict range
  test and stayed at mid-height; the door cut-out then left it as a sawtooth on the C-pillar (and a spike or a step on
  the far LODs). The range is inclusive now: one straight slant on every car and LOD (17 cars with slanted rear
  edges, plus 5 with rear quarter windows, checked closed and open against the previous build).
- Test rig: tools/run.sh and build.sh take a shared launch lock around their memory gate and hold it 30 s after a
  game starts (15 s after a compile) until the new process shows up in tools/memfree.sh's count. Launches in the same
  seconds (after a restart) used to pass the gate together and get one of them OOM-killed.
