Street life 4 and night interiors: the street reacts to the player's car, AI peds get in through the door, lit rooms behind glass at night, crowds that slow behind slower walkers

AI sets 14-16 (src/game: ai.cpp, ai_game.h, app.cpp, barks.cpp, pedai.cpp, police.cpp; peds.cpp gets one line):
- Set 14: the street watches the player's car.
  - Screeching tyres turn heads within 30 m, with the occasional "Whoa!".
  - Donuts or a burnout in one spot make a show: most people stop to watch and the bold film it with their phones up. The
    steadier sort tut and walk on, officers on foot call out "Hey! Knock it off!", and the crowd breaks up when it ends.
  - A car left over a crosswalk or up on a sidewalk gets glares and words ("Hey! I'm walking here!"), and the bold slap
    the hood going past.
  - People standing near a stopped car with its radio on may dance to it, or ask for it to be turned down.
- Set 15: the player on foot.
  - Sprinting past close gets looks and the odd "Jeez, slow down!".
  - Late at night on a quiet street, someone walking alone towards the player may cross to the other sidewalk, or give a
    wide berth.
- Set 16: getting into cars through the door, and held hands.
  - AI peds now walk to their seat's door and get in with the door clip (GameWorld::startCarEntry), about 3 s from the
    door to seated, and the car waits until everyone is in. This covers owners driving off, companions riding along,
    prisoners being put in, delivery drivers, road-rage and sorry drivers, ambulance crews, coffee-break officers, taxi
    fares and officers returning to their car. If a seat has no door clip, the plain enter-car clip or warp is used.
  - Couples' held hands hang fingers-down, palms facing (in.grabAxis in the couple block of peds.cpp).
- Tests (app.cpp test code): showoff, crosswalk, runby and nightwalk modes. The crosswalk test turns the radio off in
  stages 2-3.

Night renderer phase A (src/shaders/post.hlsl, src/render/post.cpp, src/shaders/facade.hlsli, PROGRESS.md):
- At night a lit lobby or shop behind glass no longer reads as a white block. A local exposure pulls large bright areas
  towards the street's exposure and keeps their detail, the way the eye adapts. Lamps, neon and distant windows stay
  bright, with no halo, and a lit window filling half the frame no longer darkens the street. It is off by day.
- Windows show rooms lit by their own lamp: a ceiling fixture, a corner table lamp, or only a TV's blue flicker.
  - Light falls off across the room.
  - Lamps run from candle-warm to cool LED: homes mostly warm, offices neutral to cool.
  - Levels spread over about three stops, and fewer homes are lit late at night.
- Shop shelves and clothing rails use muted colours instead of rainbow bars.
- The tonemap's sharpening stays within each pixel's neighbourhood range, so there is no pale line along dark brows,
  lash lines or window frames.

Animation, change 1 (src/game/peds.cpp, GameWorld::updatePeds' ped-ped separation):
- Two non-player peds pressing into each other now also lose the closing part of their relative velocity, split like the
  position push, the way movePed treats a static collider. Someone walking into another's back carries the speed they
  actually make, so their gait no longer drags planted feet into quick steps, and the AI's stuck test (aiWalkRound)
  sees them held up. Pairs with the player keep the position push only, so pushing through a crowd feels as before.

Animation, stair items 2-3 (src/anim/animator.cpp):
- Toes are kept 5.5 cm (was 4.5) plus half an update's travel clear of the next riser, so they clear it at low frame
  rates too.
- A foot landing on stairs without a fitted tread goes down at the tread under its forefoot.
