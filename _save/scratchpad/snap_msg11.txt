Shorter police and ambulance response, spray from wet skids, settled test screenshots, car door choreography

- Dispatch: police and ambulance units are sent from a lane whose way in to the scene is at most 1.7 times the
  straight line (not round a block of one-way streets first), ambulances start 120-180 m out of view, and a unit
  crawling in stop-and-go traffic counts as held up (moved past the jam out of sight after a few seconds). In the
  traffic harness three calls took 38, 21 and 10 s (38, 47 and 92 s before the response work).
- K9: the scent trail ends at a jump of over 15 m (a ride or a scripted move), so the dog never follows a trail
  straight through a teleport.
- Vehicles: a skid on wet pavement throws spray instead of tyre smoke.
- Animation: the car door choreography (reaching for the handle, the door swinging in step, ducking under the roof
  rail, the seat belt) is authored at run time from each car's door geometry; the game does not use it yet, so
  getting in and out is unchanged. The B-pillar inside the opening is 4 cm less intrusive.
- Tests: with --renderevery N a requested screenshot waits for six frames rendered in a row, so TAA, reflection,
  occlusion and fog history settle as in play; the hurt test runs 150 s; traffic_sim dispatches like the game.
