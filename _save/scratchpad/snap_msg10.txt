Opening car doors, ambulances that wait for their patient, K9 handlers that close in, far-LOD shades

- Vehicles: every full-detail car (all 27 lofted models: compacts, sedans, coupes, SUVs, pickups, muscle cars, the
  Tomo, police cars and taxis) is cut along its shut lines into the body and its doors. Closed, the car is the same as
  before, vertex for vertex; open, a door shows painted jambs, a sill with a scuff plate, the B-pillar and rubber
  seals, with its own shut faces and inner panel. The game draws each door turned about its hinge (the car stays at
  full detail within 120 m while one is open), the garage display car draws its doors, and the vehicle viewer can
  open them (-doors). Getting in and out through them comes next.
- Animation: the groundwork for getting in and out of cars through the doors (the door input, authoring of the
  enter and exit motions, two seat-belt clips); not used by the game yet.
- Hurt pedestrians: an ambulance waits on scene for a patient walking to it (up to 100 s), a patched-up patient gets
  a short limp instead of the long one, and the crew gets out only once the ambulance has stopped.
- Police: the K9 handler closes in on an unarmed suspect on foot, so the dog gets near enough to count as a find.
- Characters: far-LOD figures keep their sunglasses (the tint painted round the eyes), far eyes are less starey, and
  denim overshirts get a faded denim blue instead of near black.
