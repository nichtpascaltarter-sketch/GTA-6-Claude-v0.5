Feet stay on the stairs at every pace, traffic gets round parked vans, night car lights, and the world model

Stairs (animation agent; src/anim/animator.cpp, character.h):
- At the AI's coming stair pace (about 0.5 m/s, one tread a step), people keep their feet on the treads on straight
  flights, switchbacks, L stairs with landings and curved stairs, and on the step that turns onto a flight. Before,
  a sixth of the planted feet stood inside a step. In game at 0.5 m/s, feet 3 cm or more inside a step: up
  15.9% -> 1.2%, down 17.9% -> 2.7%.
- Jogging, running and sprinting on stairs use the run's own heel-strike lead (about 0.3 m) for their landings,
  instead of the walk's 0.3 of a stride. The player's planted frames inside a step, all paces and stairs together:
  5.8% -> 1.1%. Running up the metro stairs: 32.5% -> 0.0%.
- Walking up a switchback, the first foot after the landing goes on the next flight's first tread, not into its riser.
  A late re-scan keeps the tread already fitted unless another needs a much smaller move.
- anim_test passes. The locomotion harness, the building-kit sweeps and the in-game stair tours are in the agent's
  sign-offs (c12-c14).

Traffic (AI agent; src/game/traffic_core.cpp, ai_core.h, ai.cpp, tools/traffic_sim.cpp):
- Close pairs of junctions: a car sees the next junction's light from the lane before the first and from inside its
  box. Where the short link can't hold it at a red, it waits at the first line, for 30 s at most. At an all-way stop
  whose line is at the box's exit, it stops there and goes in the stop's order.
- A permissive left turner on green stops at its hold point until the gap check lets it go, instead of rolling into
  oncoming traffic. Two cars stopped nose to nose inside a junction box count as blocking each other up to 6 m, so
  one backs off.
- Getting round a parked van, a breakdown or an abandoned car replaces the old overtake. The car takes the smaller
  side step that clears the blocker by 0.3 m, within the room the road gives, and creeps by at 3 m/s at most. If it
  stopped too close, it backs up first. It never pulls into a lane that isn't there.
- The traffic health log names the head of a stuck queue, and the player's own car when autoplay drives it.
- Harness, 15 runs, before -> after: red runs 73 -> 2; waits over 60 s away from the overlapping roads 75 -> 3; waits
  over 30 s behind a parked car 7 -> 0. In game: soak 300 s, hwysoak, life and chase all pass.
