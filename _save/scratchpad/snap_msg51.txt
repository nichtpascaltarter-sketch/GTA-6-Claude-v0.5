Night phase C: street lamps light like road luminaires, sodium and LED blocks, aviation beacons on towers, varied office lights

Night renderer phase C (src/shaders: facade.hlsli, lights.hlsli, props.hlsl, particles.hlsl; src/render: world_render.cpp,
particles.cpp; PROGRESS.md):
- Street lamps light like road luminaires:
  - Each pool has an edge: the light peaks about 60 degrees out along the road and ends by 80 degrees.
  - Only about a third of the light falls behind the pole, so poles, pavements and the facades behind them are no longer
    floodlit by their own lamp. The motel behind its pole lamp now reads as night.
- Lamp installations change block by block: orange high-pressure sodium, warm-white, neutral and cool-white LED. Each
  lamp's lens glows in its own colour.
- Buildings over 60 m carry red aviation beacons at their roof corners. They flash in the same rhythm as the beacons on
  masts, spires and the landmarks, and pulse gently with Reduce Flashing on.
- Offices lit outside working hours have only some lights on, and office levels vary more, so downtown towers no longer
  read as walls of equally bright windows.
- Daytime is unchanged.
- facade.hlsli is a clean 3-way merge with the city agent's storefront gate (streetMark lines untouched).
