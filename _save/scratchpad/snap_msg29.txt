Locomotion: planted feet follow the body's actual move, so pushes and nudges no longer drag them

- The animator now keeps planted feet in place by the root's actual displacement since its last update (Ped::animPos,
  AnimInput::rootMove), instead of speed x dt along the move direction.
- So whatever moves the body without showing in its velocity no longer drags the planted feet:
  - the ped-ped separation push;
  - a collider's push-out;
  - a snap.
  A metre or more at once counts as a warp, and the velocity is used then. The gait speed still comes from the
  velocity.
- Step harness, slide per step:
  - a standing person pushed 1.08 m sideways: one 106 cm drag becomes small steps (0.9 cm mean, 8.6 max);
  - walking while jostled: 25.8 -> 1.3 cm mean.
  Every other scenario is unchanged to the digit.
- In game (tour stop 2 downtown, deterministic):
  - slide per step: mean 7.42 -> 6.76 cm, p90 14.3 -> 11.6 cm, max 504 -> 385 cm;
  - standing people: mean 6.4 -> 3.5 cm, max 143 -> 33 cm.
  Close passes are unchanged. Group followers' remaining slides come from their steering, which the AI's next set
  fixes.
- Tests: anim_test ALL PASSED and the MinGW syntax check is clean. Peds CPU is unchanged, within noise.
