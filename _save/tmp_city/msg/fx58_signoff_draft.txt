2. Pads people and vehicles stand on (blocksbin.h/.cpp, roads.cpp, cellgen.cpp).
   - HandBlocks::padHeight (inline in blocksbin.h, so the tools that build cells still link without blocksbin.cpp): the top of the level ground covering a point, the highest at or below maxZ. The level ground is the flat polygons: pads, decks, levelled parcels and their paint.
   - levelByCell indexes each one by every cell its outline's box reaches (edges included, 5 cm past them), like the props' index but not by the centroid alone. A pad across a cell's edge answers on both sides.
   - RoadNetwork::surfaceHeight consults it after the sites' pads, only when gHandBlocks is set. GameWorld::groundHeight -> CollisionWorld::ground -> surfaceHeight then stands peds and vehicles on a pad within their step, and its edge is a step like a kerb's.
   - The procedural world has no gHandBlocks, so nothing changes there (census below).
   - cellgen draws a raised level polygon's sides, where it stands 10 cm or more above the terrain, from its top down into the ground (wood for a deck, concrete otherwise). The step is then seen as well as felt.
