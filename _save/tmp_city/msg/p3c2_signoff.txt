HOW
- tools/author/street.py, for parcels whose outline runs 0.5 m in under their streets (the ground slice):
  - The face's front line is the parcels' fronts plus that 0.5 m (UNDER) plus the 0.6 m setback. So the buildings stand 0.6 m behind the sidewalk, as in batch 1.
  - A corner plan's side wall stands UNDER + 0.05 in from the parcel's side, which is 0.05 m behind the side street's sidewalk.
  - With --ground, every footprint corner, taken 0.25 m in, must stand on its parcel's level ground (at most 0.08 m over its grade's ground). The raised-floor check samples 0.25 m in from the edges too, half the grid's step.
  - I dumped the ground grids at 0.25 m. At 0.5 m the sidewalk's edge smeared over a cell, and the corner shop by Avenida del Sol was refused.
  - Without these changes the corner shops would have stood 0.45 m out on the side streets' sidewalks, with floors raised up to 0.07 m.
  - b006's files are as batch 1 wrote them. A re-run of street.py on b006 would now move its front line, because the ground slice moved its parcels' outlines.
- tools/author/dress.py (new in the tree): my pilot script, with its header updated for its inputs. The block files' marker keeps its wording, since each file is split on it.
  - The fix: with_tees no longer re-inserts a parcel's own vertex.
