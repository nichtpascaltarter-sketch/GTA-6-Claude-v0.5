What it does:
1a. The pilot's streets (world/): world.ntw, districts/cl/district.ntw and roads.ntw, and lib/signals/cl_twophase.ntw.
  - 52 nodes, 83 edges, 8.9 km of street.

1b. The terrain is graded under the roads.
  - Two mechanical moves, so worldc can run today's grading:
    - worldmap.cpp's grid queries and grading primitives go to terrainops.cpp, which worldmap.cpp includes.
    - roads.cpp's grading pass goes to roadgrade.cpp: gradeTerrainForRoads, declared in roads.h.
  - New: the grading leaves authored decks alone.
  - worldc grades the baked heights and stores a delta in "terrain.grade", 3 KB for the pilot. The game adds it at load, before the splat.
  - A compile fix: an edge's polyline now gets a point at every profile break. Before, a straight shape with a deck profile came out flat, and the pilot's bridge did. There is a new test for it.
1c. Hand mode draws the road cells.
  - generateCell builds the roads without the procedural dressing (roadmesh.cpp: a dressing switch; works zones off in hand mode), and nothing else procedural.
  - In app.cpp, game.init runs when the hand world has roads. Hand mode never opens the main menu; --play and --autoplay start a new game, and otherwise you get the free camera.
  - In savegame.cpp, saveGame and loadGame return false in hand mode, so no hand session ever touches the real saves.
- Docs: HANDBUILT_WORLD 6.1 and NTW_FORMAT section 1 say what worldc compiles now.
- Tests: worldmodel_test adds testProfilePoints and testGrade (a street's cut, a bridge's ramps, a deck over a hill and a river).
