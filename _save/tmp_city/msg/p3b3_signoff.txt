WHAT (player-facing)
- In hand mode, each of b005's 12 buildings has an interior: 6 shops with the flat over each, 2 three-storey walk-ups and 4 bungalows.
  - 120 rooms on 22 floors, 90 doors whose leaves swing, and 10 stairs.
  - No furniture or people yet.
- The player walks in through the plans' real doors:
  - the shop's glass pair door, and the flat's own street door beside it;
  - the walk-up's street door;
  - the bungalow's front door and its kitchen's side door.
- Inside, the walls, floors, stairs and door leaves are solid. A leaf swings away from whoever walks up to it, as in the generator's interiors.
- Where the ground outside is lower than the floor, a ground-floor door has a concrete stoop.
  - The cause is the 0.3-0.5 m gutter between the sidewalk and the facades.
  - The stoop is a landing level with the floor, or a landing with steps down.
- The stairs climb on a ramp of thin colliders, three to a tread, as the warehouse mezzanine was meant to. The player walks up and down them, and the treads are drawn as boxes.
  - The shops' flights are straight and wooden.
  - The walk-ups' flights are terrazzo doglegs with landings, and their wells have balustrades.
- The third-person camera stays under the ceilings inside (QUESTIONS 2).
- Lights:
  - each room has a fixture by its type (a pendant, downlight or troffer);
  - its lamps and ambient follow its windows' light state, from the same function as the facade shader: a window lit from the street is a lit room inside, and both go dark at the same minute.
- Party walls (m01|s02, s03|s04, s05|m06, s07|s08): above the lower building's roof the higher one's shared wall shows, and so do its trims; below that it is hidden. Before this fix, the walk-up's facade showed inside the shop.
- Nothing changes in procedural mode, and world.bin is byte-identical to 75's.

HOW
- New src/world/interiorhand.cpp. interiors.h adds IK_HAND, prepareHandInteriors, handInteriorTour and handRoomLight.
  - At load (handworld.cpp, after prepareHandBuildings), each placement whose plan has rooms gets an InteriorDef. They sit in a set of hand mode's own, which gInteriors points at. Each def holds:
    - its frame: the origin at the middle of the front wall's centreline, x the plan's (mirrored where the placement is);
    - its rooms as boxes: the polygon's bounds less half the walls round them (a stair's room reaches the floor above);
    - its doors with leaves: hinged, or a pair for the shop's glass door. Hinge end and swing come from 3b-2's records, mirrored where the placement is. An `opening` has no leaf;
    - daylight portals through its windows and exterior doors;
    - the markers outside and inside its main entrance;
    - each room's light key.
  - It never goes through planInteriors or pickBuilding (your note), and Building::interior stays -1.
  - The existing buildInterior builds its mesh, collision and lights on a worker. It does so through a hook in interiorlayouts.cpp's runLayout (gLayoutHand), so tools that compile interiorlayouts.cpp without interiorhand.cpp still link.
  - layoutHand builds:
    - the exterior walls' inner faces, with the windows' inner reveals, glass and window boards;
    - the inside walls with their door casings (collided);
    - floors and ceilings with the stairwells cut out, and the upper floors' slabs (collided);
    - per stair: a drawn box per tread; the ramp, 3 colliders per tread, their tops on the line through the treads' middles (within a third of a riser of the tread); the landings; the well's edges; and 1 m balustrades where a floor stops at a well;
    - baseboards (none across a well);
    - a light per room by its type.
  - handRoomLight: a room's lamps are roomLight(class, seed, windows, day, minute, night) / 222.
    - The class and room hash come from its window's facade entry, as facade.hlsl takes them: a shop's shaderHash3(2000, 77, seed), others' shaderHash3(1000, 1, seed).
    - A room with no window uses its own seed.
- interiors_game.cpp:
  - collision keys: an IK_HAND interior's colliders and leaves get positive keys (2000000 + def*256 + sub). The generator's keep their negative ones (QUESTIONS 1).
  - IK_HAND lamps and ambient use handRoomLight, not the schedules; submitAll now takes the game day.
  - the light budget: the renderer keeps 32 ambient volumes, nearest interior first.
    - A hand-built interior's lamps light only when all its rooms got a volume this frame.
    - A lamp outside every volume acts as an outdoor light, so it would light the street and the neighbours through the walls.
  - --handtour <names>|all (with --play --autoplay handtour) walks the player along handInteriorTour:
    - from outside the main entrance, through every door and up every stair, into each room;
    - it logs each room reached, and each waypoint not reached within 6 s (it then puts the player there and counts it);
    - it takes a shot per floor and on each stair. A stair's room has no middle waypoint, since that point is on its flight or over its well.
- handshells.cpp, for a shell whose plan has rooms:
  - Its doorways are open through the wall at the near LOD, over a stone threshold, and the interior hangs the leaves.
  - Its collision is hollow, where it was one box: the foundation up to the ground floor, each exterior wall floor by floor with door gaps and lintels, and the roof slab.
  - Stoops (buildStoops): the walk steps onto a box only within 0.35 m of its feet (physics.cpp capsuleOverlap), so the 0.47 m gutter blocked every door.
  - Party walls:
    - prepareHandBuildings finds, for each wall and outline edge, how high a neighbour hides its outer face (HandBuildings wallCover and edgeCover, runtime only, in buildingsbin.h);
    - buildWall clips the face and its openings below that;
    - the bands, cornice, coping and quoins skip the covered edges and corners.
- worldmodel_buildings.cpp stairEnd (Codex P2): +1: and -1: name the nearest floor above or below by z, with worldv's 0.02 tolerance, as validate_layout.cpp's neighbour() does. Before, they followed record order (fi + k).
- Docs: HANDBUILT_WORLD 7.2 (interiors, hollow collision, stoops, party walls), 7.3, 7.4 and 7.8 (the light state); NTW_FORMAT 9.10 (relative ends by height).
- camera.cpp (QUESTIONS 2): inside a hand-built building (Interiors::insideHandBuilt, interiors_game.cpp), the on-foot camera's lift above the ground no longer passes up through a collider's underside. It tests that with a colliders-only raycast up.

QUESTIONS (yours to decide; 3b-3 doesn't depend on them)
1. A bug in main: no generator interior collides.
   - interiors_game.cpp's colKeyFor gives every interior a negative collision key, and CollisionWorld stores the key as Collider::owner.
   - ground(), capsuleOverlap(), raycast() and boxContacts() skip owner < 0, which means freed. So none of the generator's interiors' colliders or door leaves has ever collided, since the interiors pipeline (5b5c080).
   - Their walls, furniture and leaves are walk-through, and the warehouse mezzanine's stairs can't be climbed. Only the hollow shells, which are in the cell collision, hold.
   - 3b-3 fixes this for IK_HAND only: positive keys from 2000000. The one-line fix for all interiors would change procedural play: walls, furniture, leaves and the mezzanine would become solid for the player, the NPCs and the vehicles.
   - With it, camera.cpp's new ceiling rule (below) should apply to every interior. A separate slice by me, or the interiors/physics owner's?
2. The camera, which 3b-3 changes in hand mode only.
   - The on-foot camera lifts itself to the ground probe + 0.35. The probe finds collider tops up to 1.05 m over the camera.
   - With solid slabs, a camera near a ceiling (pitch below about -7 degrees) popped through to the floor above or onto the roof.
   - Inside a hand-built building, it no longer lifts through a collider's underside. Outside them, and in procedural mode, the code path is the old one.
3. Locks and hours: the plans' lock= (resident, staff, key) and the shop's hours= aren't compiled or enforced, so every door opens for anyone. OK for 3b, or wanted before 3c?
4. The light budget:
   - the renderer keeps 32 ambient volumes, so around b005 only the nearest 1-3 buildings' lamps light (a walk-up has 21 rooms);
   - the windows still show their light state from the street;
   - raising kMaxInteriorVolumes is a renderer change, if wanted.
