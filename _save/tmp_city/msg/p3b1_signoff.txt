WHAT (player-facing)
- In hand mode the 12 buildings are buildings to the game, not only to the renderer and the physics. Peds' spawn and flee checks, window-shopping, traffic, police, wildlife, missions and the map now see them through pointInBuilding, buildingsNear and the rest.
- With your change: when a ped steps out of one of them, or walks into one, it uses the plan's real main entrance. That is the shop's glass pair door, the walk-up's street door and the bungalow's front door, never the middle of the facade (a walk-up's window).
- Nothing is drawn differently. The procedural world is unchanged.

HOW
- The main entrance is now compiled. worldmodel reads the plans' entrance records, and the compiler takes the entrance of kind main, else the first. The shell stores it as an exterior wall and an opening on it ("library" section v3: 8 bytes more per shell). The reader refuses an entrance that is not a door, or that lies past the walls.
- At load (handshells.cpp), each placement gets a Building record in the building set and in its cell's list. The record holds:
  - the walls' outer box, base z, the walls' top as its height, and floors;
  - style from the plan's class (the class list is in BuildingStyle's order; landmark maps to tower);
  - front = the plan's -y, no facade entry, no interior;
  - a seed ending in 7, so the population parks no car on a driveway that isn't there;
  - its own box as its lot;
  - doorAlong = the main entrance's offset from the box's centre along the front.
- Building has two new fields: doorAlong, and noStreetDoor for a main entrance that is not on the front wall (none of the 12).
- aiBuildingDoorNear and aiBuildingDoorAway (ai.cpp) put the door at c + ax*doorAlong. For procedural buildings doorAlong is 0, the same point as before.
- Docs: NTW_FORMAT 9.10 (what the compiler reads) and HANDBUILT_WORLD 7.2.
