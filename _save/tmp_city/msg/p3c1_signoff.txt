HOW
- tools/author/street.py (new, Python, run with -I): HANDBUILT 5.3's street script.
  - It reads the block's parcels, the district's streets and the library's plans and palettes.
  - It writes one building file per placement, as b005's are. A re-run rewrites only the files that still carry its line.
  - The rules:
    - Street wall on an avenue, a house in the middle of each parcel on a street.
    - A corner plan where another street runs by a parcel's end.
    - Mirrors alternate, no palette twice side by side, no plan with the same palette twice within three, no house plan twice in neighbouring parcels.
    - Every footprint corner inside its parcel. The front line steps back up to 0.9 m where a parcel's cut or a crooked front asks for it, as at p08's triangle.
  - z: 0.01 m over the sidewalk in front, from the street's profile and kerb.
    - With --ground, it also checks the ground under the footprint. That is a grid of the compiled ground, which I dumped from world.bin with a scratch host tool.
  - Seed 6. A re-run gives the same files.
- handshells.cpp:
  - buildStoops' flush test reads the ground 0.05, 0.15 and 0.3 m out, not 0.15 m alone, and the landing's search starts at 0.15 m.
  - buildHandBuildingsCell logs each cell it builds: placements, triangles, ms.
- interiors_game.cpp: the "Interior ... built" line also gives the interiors resident, how many are hand-built, and their triangles.
- docs: HANDBUILT_WORLD 5.3 (the script), 7.2 (the stoop's rule) and 7.4 (the 33 interiors, the logs).

FOUND, NOT FIXED (pre-existing; for you to decide)
- Junction corners have no sidewalk in the ground query, in hand mode. This is the larger of the two.
  - At a junction, the corner sidewalk squares between the approaches' sidewalks are drawn, but the ground query (roadnet.cpp roadSurfaceHeight) leaves them out. It drops an approach's sidewalk band inside the junction's cut-backs ("the corner sidewalks are the junction's own"), and the junction disc covers only its radius.
  - So the query falls through to the terrain, which in hand mode is the natural ground, 0.45 m lower.
  - Measured on the ground dump:
    - At Calle Luna x Avenida Palma (cl.n0025, four ways), each of the four corners has a ~4 by 4 m patch at 3.21 under a sidewalk drawn at 3.66.
    - At the T at cl.n0033, Calle Luna's south sidewalk is a hole from x 1731 to 1749.
  - The walk drops into a patch and cannot climb its 0.45 m edge, so a pedestrian or the player rounding a corner on foot can be stuck until they jump.
  - The procedural world has the same code, but its terrain is graded to road level there, so it sinks 0.15 m and doesn't trap.
  - What I'd do, as one small hand-only slice: worldc grades the terrain under every junction's corner squares to the sidewalk's height, as it grades under the roads. world.bin's terrain delta changes; procedural mode, the census and the query code do not.
  - The other way, giving the corners a surface in roadSurfaceHeight, changes the procedural census's road queries.
  - Your call.
- Parcels are not graded, so the yards lie 0.4-0.45 m below the sidewalks. This holds for b005 and b006 alike, since dress.py writes no grade=.
  - Every door has its stoop, and every room is reached from the street.
  - But stepping off the sidewalk into a side or back yard is a drop, and coming back needs a jump.
  - What I'd do: dress.py gives the mixed and residential parcels grade= at their sidewalk's height, with a blend. That is a change to all 25 blocks' generated parts. It can come with the corner fix or later.
