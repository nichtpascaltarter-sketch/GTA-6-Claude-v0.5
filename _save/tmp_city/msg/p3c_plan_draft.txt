Where 3c stands
- Calle Luna has 25 blocks with ~325 parcels. b005 holds 12 placements; the other 24 blocks hold ~427.
- The library has 3 plans: a shop with flats over it, a walk-up and a bungalow.
- Uses: blocks b002-b007 are mixed, b008-b013 are mixed and residential, b014-b025 are commercial and residential, and b001 is a park.

Authoring script: tools/author/street.py (HANDBUILT 5.3). It is Python, run with -I, never shipped, and its output is the data.
- Input: a block's parcels (block.ntw), the district's plan list, and rules. The rules:
  - the plan fits the parcel's frontage and depth and suits its use;
  - no plan twice within 3 lots, and no palette twice side by side;
  - mirrors alternate, so neighbours share party walls;
  - corner parcels get corner plans once there are some.
- Output: one building file per placement, as b005's are. Each has the plan, at, z (the ground at the front), yaw (the street edge), mirror, palette and options, and back buildings go at the parcel's rear.
- It prints its seed and a summary per block: placements per plan, frontage used, and any parcel it skipped and why.
- I review the result and edit the text by hand where needed. Nothing is generated at load.
