city snapshot-safe: dead ends' turning bulbs, the mesh fix you asked for. The bulb's sidewalk ring and kerb no longer cross the street's mouth, and the street's sidewalks, kerbs, skirts, rails and paint stop where they meet the bulb. Both worlds. On 0e4bf3a with my three accepted for 68; the three files sit on I-38's roadmesh.cpp and the written bulbs' roadnet.cpp and worldmodel_test.cpp.

WHAT (player-facing)
- Every dead end with a turning bulb: the procedural world's 1,124 (two-way lanes, streets and rural roads), and the pilot's 5.
  - Before, the street was drawn all the way to the node, sidewalks, kerbs, skirts, rails and centre line included, and the bulb's disc and full sidewalk ring were drawn on top. So a 0.15 m kerb ring ran across the street's mouth, and the street's two sidewalks ran on inside the turning circle as raised strips.
  - Now the ring is open across the mouth, and its two ends meet the street's two sidewalks: the keyhole shape a cul-de-sac has. The street's kerbs, sidewalks, skirts and rails stop where they meet the ring, and its paint stops at the disc's edge.
- The ground the game stands people and cars on agrees (roadnet.cpp roadSurfaceHeight):
  - across the mouth it is the street's carriageway, not the ring's 0.15 m;
  - inside the disc the street's sidewalks no longer count.
  - The lane graph's walk ring already stopped the walk at the ring.

HOW
- roadmesh.cpp:
  - bulbCross(): where one of the street's lines leaves a circle round the dead end's node, measured on the same sections the edge's quads are drawn on. Both the edge and the node use it, so their meeting points are the same to the bit.
  - The edge: per end and side, the kerb line's meeting point with the disc (radius r) and the outer line's (the sidewalk's, else the kerb line) with the ring's outer edge (r + the ring's sidewalk).
    - A quad wholly on the street's side of those is drawn exactly as before.
    - The quads they cut get the curb face and skirt along their lines' stretches, and the sidewalk top clipped by the line between the two meeting points.
    - Shoulder skirts stop at the kerb line's meeting point.
    - Rail sub-spans inside the ring are left out, and the paint stops at the disc.
  - The node: the ring runs from one side's two meeting points round the far side to the other's, at the street's height at its ends. When a street is as wide as its bulb, there are no meeting points, and the ring is drawn all round as before.
- roadnet.cpp: inside a bulb's disc, an edge's sidewalk band doesn't count. The ring's 0.15 m doesn't apply within the dead-end street's carriageway (inCarriageway).
- Test (worldmodel_test), on the written 8 m bulb of the test fixture's dead end:
  - the mouth at 9.25 m is road, not +0.15 (66: +0.16);
  - a point on the street's sidewalk band inside the disc is road (66: +0.15);
  - the ring beside the mouth is still +0.15.
  - The test fails on 66 and passes with the change.
