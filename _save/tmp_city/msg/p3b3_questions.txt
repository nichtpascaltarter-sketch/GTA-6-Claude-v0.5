QUESTIONS (yours to decide; 3b-3 doesn't depend on them)
1. A bug in main: no generator interior collides.
   - interiors_game.cpp's colKeyFor gives every interior a negative collision key, and CollisionWorld stores the key as Collider::owner.
   - ground(), capsuleOverlap(), raycast() and boxContacts() skip owner < 0, which means freed. So none of the generator's interiors' colliders or door leaves has ever collided, since the interiors pipeline (5b5c080).
   - Their walls, furniture and leaves are walk-through, and the warehouse mezzanine's stairs can't be climbed. Only the hollow shells, which are in the cell collision, hold.
   - 3b-3 fixes this for IK_HAND only: positive keys from 2000000. The one-line fix for all interiors would change procedural play: walls, furniture, leaves and the mezzanine would become solid for the player, the NPCs and the vehicles.
   - With it, camera.cpp's new ceiling rule (below) should apply to every interior. A separate slice by me, or the interiors/physics owner's?

   - Inside a hand-built building, it no longer lifts through a collider's underside. Outside them, and in procedural mode, the code path is the old one.
