ONE THING FOR YOU TO DECIDE (I changed nothing)
- The Avenida faces' colours repeat from block to block.
  - b014, b015 and b016 each run coral, mint, sky, sand, rose from their west corner.
  - b014's and b016's faces have the same ten colours in order, and the same plans for the first seven.
- The cause: street.py draws every block from seed 6, and these faces come out of the same draws. Calle Luna's faces already do the same (b002 and b011 start alike).
- A fix: give the palettes a seed of their own per block, from the block id. The palette draw is street.py's last use of the generator, so this would break the repeats without moving a building or changing a plan.
- The catch: it would recolour every building street.py wrote in rows 1-3. So I've left it for you or the user.
