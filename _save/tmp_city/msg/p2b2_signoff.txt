HOW
- "blocks.walk":
  - paths: id, kind (walk, plaza, alley, trail, boardwalk, footbridge, underpass), width, polyline xyz on the ground. A footbridge or underpass takes its heights from its profile (absolute), with a point at each of the profile's s.
  - plazas: id, z (NaN: on the ground), polygon, tags.
  - steps: bottom and top xyz, width, treads and rise. Each end stands on the level ground: a pad or levelled parcel under it, else the ground. Treads and rise are kept as written, else derived from the height between the ends, with a rise near 0.17 m.
- "blocks.spots": per spot its kind (NTW_FORMAT 9.5's 34 kinds, outdoors and in), flags (implied, cover), id, at xyz and facing (dx, dy, unit; 0,0 for any way), the queue's line xyz (head first), cap, hours (minutes of the day, from and to per range; a range may run past midnight), roles, ages (min-max), prop (an index into "blocks"' props, or none), pose and for (as text).
  - Written spots: an at= or a line=, not both. A queue faces its head and holds a place every 0.75 m unless cap= says. hours= is h.mm-h.mm ranges joined by ";". prop= is the nearest prop of that piece within 2 m. Each stands on the ground, or on its prop's base.
  - Implied, after the written ones (flagged implied):
    - A seat on every prop that has one, from catalogcodes.h seatSpec (as propmesh.cpp builds them): a bench's seat is 1.8 m wide and 0.48 m up; a bus shelter's bench is 2.4 m wide and 0.6 m behind its front. It faces the piece's front, with a place every 0.6 m.
    - A shelter within 15 m of a bus stop becomes that stop's wait: busstop_wait, for = the stop's id, under cover.
    - Every other bus stop on a compiled edge gets a wait by the kerb, at its wait= or else in the middle of its sidewalk at its at=, facing the street, for 6.
    - Café seats come once terraces exist.
- compileBlocks now records each prop's dressing record (BlocksOut::propDress), so the seats and prop= find their pieces. Every spot height is above the ground until placeOnTerrain, like the props.
- readBlocks reads both sections, all or nothing, and refuses a spot naming a prop the blocks don't have. worldc logs "62 spots (62 implied)" and the game's log line counts paths and spots.
