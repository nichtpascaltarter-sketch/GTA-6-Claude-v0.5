HOW
- src/core/jobs.h and jobs.cpp: a new Jobs::submitBatch(fns, prio).
  - It pushes the jobs in their order under one lock, and counts g_lowCount per job, which pendingLow() reads.
  - Then it wakes min(N, workers) workers, one notify_one each, and leaves fns empty.
  - With no workers it runs the jobs inline, in order, as submit() does.
- src/render/world_render.cpp:
  - requestCell() pushes each new cell's job onto a member, requestBatch, instead of submitting it. inFlight is counted there, at request time, as before.
  - update() hands the whole batch to Jobs::submitBatch(requestBatch, kJobLow) on the line right after the request loop. The loop is the only caller and has no other way out, so every job counted in inFlight is queued before anything else in the frame runs.
- So a frame admits at most maxInFlight minus inFlight cells. Nothing else is touched: not maxInFlight, the rings, the upload budget, or the upload order.
- HANDBUILT's streaming list gets the line "A frame's new cells go to the job queue together, under one lock and with at most one wake per worker (`Jobs::submitBatch`), with up to 6 cells in flight."
- Size: jobs.cpp +19 lines, jobs.h +3, world_render.cpp +6 and -2.
