WHAT (player-facing)
- In hand mode, b005 now has its buildings, the first hand-built ones in the game:
  - along Calle Luna, a street front of 8 in pairs on party walls, with narrow gaps at the lot lines: six two-storey shops with a flat over each, in pastels (coral, mint, lemon, rose), and a three-storey walk-up on the west corner and another in the middle;
  - behind them, four bungalows at the back of the lots, facing their yards.
- Up close:
  - stucco walls on a painted plinth or a stone base; bands at the floor lines, cornices along the parapets, and quoins on the walk-ups;
  - real window and door openings with reveals and sills;
  - the windows by their kind: double-hung under Bahama shutters on the shops' flats, casements on the walk-ups, double-hung between fixed shutters on the bungalows;
  - storefronts with glass shop doors, and panel doors;
  - flat roofs behind parapets, and the bungalows' shingled gables with their eaves.
- At night each window shows the room behind it, lit by that room's own schedule:
  - the shops are lit until 21:00, with the facade shader's shop interiors behind the glass (clothing shops among them in the shots);
  - the flats' living rooms light up in the evening and the bedrooms later;
  - the walk-ups' stairs and corridors stay lit;
  - the windows of one room light up together.
- The buildings are solid: each adds one collision box to its cell, as the generated buildings do, and the doors stay shut until the interiors come.

HOW
- The hand-built window flag, option (b):
  - FacadeGPU::flags bit 6 (64) is kFacadeHandWindow, and the room's class rides in bits 8-11. Bits 0-5 were taken (5 is your parking flag); bit 7 and bits 12-31 stay free.
  - The bits are documented in facade.hlsli, in buildings.h's flag list, and in roomlight.h with its HLSL copy.
  - facade.hlsli takes one line: the class is handWindowSchedule(flags) when the bit is set, else facadeRoomSchedule as before.
  - Pinned:
    - roomlighttest.hlsl's vectors now take facade flags. 2 hand-window vectors (a living room's, and a storefront shop's) carry their class in the flags with the schedule set to never, and both the GPU and the C++ must light them as their class.
    - worldmodel_test checks the flags' round trip for every class, and that they stay clear of bits 0-5 and bit 7.
  - Procedural facades are unchanged byte for byte. The census's hash of the facade table is 062c9cd's, and none of its 42,219 entries uses a flag past bit 5.
- src/world/handshells.cpp (new):
  - It is included by cellgen.cpp (as buildings.cpp includes blockstyle.cpp), so the tools that build cells get it.
  - At load, prepareHandBuildings:
    - finds each placement's cell (by its footprint's centre);
    - appends to the building set a facade entry for each window and glass door, which the renderer uploads unchanged. An entry is one window cell the size of the glass, flagged a hand-built window with the room's class; a storefront entry for a shop's windows and doors; the office flag for an office.
    - gives a room's windows one seed, so they show the same room and light together.
    - b005 has 96 entries.
  - The near LOD:
    - the walls around their openings; the base; a foundation down to 0.3 m under the lowest ground;
    - the reveals, and the glass 10 cm in;
    - the door leaves (panel, glass, roll-up, garage), sills, glazing bars by the window's kind, and fixed or Bahama shutters;
    - the look's trims;
    - the roof: flat behind a parapet with its coping, or gable, hip or shed with overhang, fascia and soffit.
  - The far LOD keeps the walls, the glass flush with them, the doors and the roofs.
  - Triangles: about 830 per building near (9,992 for b005's 12) and 150 far (1,820), inside 7.2's near-shell budget of 1,000-1,500 a house.
  - Collision: one box per building, the walls' bounds from the foundation to the roof, at both LODs.
- Compile changes, on 3a-1's format:
  - each opening carries its room (its index among the layout's rooms);
  - a door carries the schedule of the room it opens into from outside (leads=out:<room>), so a glass shop door is lit like its shop;
  - the "library" section is version 2 (an opening is 28 bytes).
