FOR THE AI AGENT: the per-side kerbs and sidewalks, a proposal, so that we both code the same numbers.
1. roads.h gets two RoadEdge accessors, halfWidthSide(side) and sidewalkSide(side). side is -1 for left and +1 for right, looking fwd.
   - In the procedural world they equal halfWidth and sidewalk exactly, so code that switches to them stays bit-identical there.
   - In hand mode, handworld sets them from EdgeExtra:
     - a side's kerb line = laneWidth * (lanesF + lanesB) / 2 + median / 2 + that side's kerb, which is halfWidth minus (the wider kerb minus this side's). That holds for one-way edges and equal counts; 6 covers unequal ones;
     - a side's sidewalk runs from its kerb line out by that side's width; 0 means none.
   - Parked cars on a side sit in [kerb line - parking, kerb line].
2. At junctions, each corner uses the side of each approach that faces it. An approach leaving the node uses its L as its left, looking out from the node. One arriving there uses its R.
3. Dead-end bulbs stay round, on the symmetric halfWidth and sidewalk.
4. roadnet's own queries (roadSurfaceHeight, onPavement, nearRoad) take the side the point is on. Kerb heights and the walk surface then agree with what is drawn.
5. In the pilot, only the Paseo del Rio differs side to side. Its 7 edges park on the left only, so their right kerb comes in 2.40 m, and the 4 m right sidewalk moves with it. Every other sidewalk is the same on both sides. I'll regenerate the 2c blocks beside those edges against the per-side footprint, in the same snapshot.
6. Two-way edges with lanesF != lanesB, which the pilot doesn't have: lanes.cpp lays each direction out from the shape, but halfWidth (and so the drawn asphalt) is centred as if the lanes were. On a 2/1 avenue with a 0.5 m shoulder, the outer fwd lane overhangs the kerb by 1.15 m. My proposal: the shape is the line between the directions, as in lanes.cpp, and each side's kerb line follows that side's lanes (median / 2 + laneWidth * that side's lanes + its kerb). The network's halfWidth becomes the wider side's, so footprint queries stay conservative. With equal counts it is today's formula, so no content changes. If you agree, I'll write it into NTW_FORMAT and the overrides test with the per-side change.
