HOW
- Catalog: body=WxDxH is an optional piece field, the solid part the collider takes where that isn't the whole piece; without it, size= is used. The pilot catalog gives it to:
  - its 5 trees: 0.52 m, the physics' trunk;
  - the street light: 0.34 m, the physics' pole;
  - the cafe umbrella: its 0.10 m pole. The physics has no umbrella collider.
- worldv's new check piece.body: body= is WxDxH and fits inside size=. It comes with a bad fixture (catalog_body.ntw).
- "blocks.colliders": per collider its kind (box or cylinder), prop (an index into "blocks"' props), centre, bottom and top z, half extents, and a box's width axis.
  - A prop gets one when its piece has collide=box or cylinder: body= or else size=, times the prop's scale. A box turns with the prop. Each stands from the prop's base up (placeOnTerrain).
  - readBlocks refuses a collider naming a prop the blocks don't have.
- HandBlocks::colliderNear(p, margin, height) is inline and looks up by cell. siteColliderNear asks it first. The procedural world has no hand blocks, so nothing changes there.
- The lot sits in b005's hand-written part above dress.py's marker, so a regeneration keeps it. Its rows give aisle= so the aisles run on 5 to 10 m past the bays, and the two rows on each aisle share it.
- worldc and the game's log line count the colliders.
