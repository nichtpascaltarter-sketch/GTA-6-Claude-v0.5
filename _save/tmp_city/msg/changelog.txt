Player-facing changelog (ordinary city blocks):
- Every ordinary building now gets a real-world architectural type from its district's palette (about 35 types): 1920s two-storey shop blocks with cornices, Mediterranean-revival shops under barrel-tile pent roofs, MiMo storefronts with cantilevered canopy slabs, corner bodegas with side-wall murals, colonnaded arcades (Grove, Calle Luna avenues), island galleries (Key Solano, Ten Palms); brick walk-ups with fire escapes and water tanks, MiMo garden apartments round courtyards, Med-revival apartments with corner towers, 1960s office slabs on pilotis, contemporary terraced mixed-use, brick lofts; 1970s balcony slabs, glass condos, MiMo resort hotels and podium condos on Collins; streamline-moderne and Med-revival hotels among the classic deco; ranch, bungalow, Mediterranean, colonial two-storey, MiMo, split-level and conch houses; modern, Mediterranean-revival and colonial villas; Ten Palms stilt houses; sawtooth sheds, office-front warehouses and body shops.
- Footprints are carved inside each lot: L and U plans, courtyards, stepped fronts and backs, split lots, corner towers, chamfered corners, rounded and curved ends, wings, podium-and-slab. Roofs: parapets, eave slabs, barrel-tile hips and pents, roof terraces, metal gables, sawtooth, butterfly; flat roofs in white, gravel, silver or tar.
- Each street side has a character (one type favoured, one avoided, a building rarely repeats its neighbour's type); corner lots get corner buildings; colours and wear vary per building, with district colour (Caribbean colours in Calle Luna and the Flats, loud painted walls in the Canvas District, pastels on Sol Beach and in North Porto Sol, conch colours on the keys).
- Street level: arcades with arches, arched window heads, streamline eyebrows, MiMo open walkways, murals, loading doors, canopies, porches (conch porches now walkable with steps), colonial porticos.
- Block middles fill in: back houses, garage rows and sheds behind the street fronts, rear parking lots with stalls, lamps and dumpsters, fenced yards and paved service yards.
- Towers get district looks (blue, teal, silver, bronze or dark glass; white ribbon-window slabs; precast; granite; white and pastel balcony towers on Collins) and some special crowns.
