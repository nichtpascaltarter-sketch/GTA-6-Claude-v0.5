What it does:
- The world model types the block records:
  - a parcel's grade=, blend= and ground=, and its paint, pad and fence children (fence gates as <at>:<width>);
  - plazas;
  - trees, plants, props and lights (a light's at= is x,y on the ground or x,y,z);
  - the catalog pieces' code=, size=WxDxH, variants= and collide=.
- compileBlocks (worldmodel_blocks.cpp, declared in worldmodel.h).
  - The ground: each parcel's ground= over its polygon, then its paint, level on its grade= where it has one, else draped on the terrain. Then its pads, as level slabs above the grade (or above the terrain under their centre), and the plazas.
  - The dressing: each piece becomes a prop of its code=, a PropType's name (catalogcodes.h). Trees are scaled to h= by the piece's size= height, and plants by scale=. Trees and plants without yaw= turn, and pick among the piece's variants, by their position, so a row never repeats.
  - Lights: each is the fixture's prop plus its lamp (catalogcodes.h lampSpec). The street light is the one roads draw today; porch, wall flood and garden lamps are lamps alone. sched= maps to roomlight.h's classes: dusk (photocell), evening (RS_PORCH), always; never leaves the fixture dark.
  - Findings: block.shape, block.kind and block.size.
- world.bin "blocks" v1 (blocksbin.h/.cpp). readBlocks never leaves half the blocks, and indexes each cell's share.
- worldc:
  - block and catalog files go through the world model.
  - The parcels' grades are graded first (WorldMap::flattenPoly, in terrainops.cpp), then today's road grading, so the roads' cut still wins at the kerb.
  - Every prop and lamp goes on the graded ground, or on a sidewalk's surface where one runs: roadSurfaceHeight is split out of surfaceHeight in roadnet.cpp, without the sites' pads.
- The game in hand mode: handworld reads "blocks", and generateCell adds each cell's share.
  - The ground is triangulated at every LOD. Draped polygons are split to 4 m in detail cells, so they follow the terrain.
  - Props and lamps go in detail cells, as today's do.
- gHandBlocks is an inline variable, so the tools that build cells (traffic_sim, worldcheck, terrainbake, my census) link without blocksbin.cpp.
- Content:
  - world/lib/catalog.ntw: 22 pieces, each naming today's enum.
  - world/districts/cl/blocks/b010/block.ntw, Domino Park at Calle Luna and the Paseo de los Heroes: lawn and paving, 4 royal palms, 3 live oaks, shrubs, 5 benches, 2 umbrellas, bins, 4 garden lights, and on Calle Luna's north sidewalk 2 cobra street lights, a hydrant and a news box: 24 props and 6 lamps.
- Docs: NTW_FORMAT section 1 and "What the blocks compiler reads"; HANDBUILT_WORLD 6.1.
- Tests (worldmodel_test):
  - the samples' block typing;
  - testBlocks: the ground's kinds and heights, the props' types, scales and yaws, the lamps' heads and schedules, the findings, the section's round trip, damaged sections, the by-cell index, and a parcel's levelling.
