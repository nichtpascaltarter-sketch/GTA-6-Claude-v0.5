WHAT (player-facing)
- Nothing visible: the same world, built faster.
- A hand cell's near build takes about 40% less time, and a far build about 60% less (numbers below).
- Every ground query in hand mode (the walk, vehicles, peds, the stoops) tests the level polygons of one 8 m tile instead of the whole 256 m cell.

HOW
- (a) blocksbin.h and .cpp: HandBlocks::padHeight looks the level ground up by 8 m tile.
  - The index: a grid of 8 m tiles over the box of all level polygons, with a metre to spare. It is 104 by 78 tiles on the pilot, with 9,225 entries. A tile grows past 8 m only where the level ground spans more than 4 km.
  - A polygon is listed in every tile its outline's box reaches, 0.05 m past its edges, in the polygons' order, as the old 256 m cell lists were built. 810 of the 1,188 level polygons span more than one tile, the largest 150.
  - The query tests that tile's polygons in that order with the old rule unchanged: skip a polygon above maxZ or not above the best so far, and the highest polygon containing the point wins. So the order couldn't change the answer anyway.
  - A point outside the grid finds nothing, as before, since the grid covers every polygon's box.
  - The old cell lists (levelByCell) are gone: padHeight was their only user. It is built at load from world.bin: no format or data change.
  - worldmodel_test's cell-edge pad test now checks the tile index, and a long low pad's middle, its far corner and past it.
- (b) render/mesh.h: MeshData::quad packs its normal and tangent once for its four corners, through addVertexPacked.
  - quad takes no normals: it computes one normal and one tangent and always gave all four corners the same ones. packNormalOct is a pure function of its input, so packing once can't change a bit.
  - Only quad changed. addVertex still packs per vertex for its other callers.
- tools/worldc.cpp: --dump-ground=<file> --ground-box=<x0,y0,x1,y1 | block id> [--ground-step=0.25].
  - After writing world.bin, worldc reads it back as hand mode does: the graded heights as loadHandTerrain forms them, readRoads with the overrides and each side's widths, and readBlocks.
  - At each grid point it takes the terrain, or the road, sidewalk or level ground over it, as RoadNetwork::surfaceHeight answers in hand mode (the roads, then the level ground; hand mode has no sites), within 3 m above the terrain and down to 0.5 m under it. That is the scratch dumper's formula.
  - A block's id boxes its parcels with 8 m around them. The world.bin it writes is byte for byte 83's.
- tools/author/street.py (comments only): --ground names worldc --dump-ground as its input. street.py reads it unchanged.
- handshells.cpp (a comment) and HANDBUILT 7.2 note your (c) for later: when a cell passes ~5 ms in the game, each placement's stoops can be worked out once at load.
- Docs: HANDBUILT 5.3 and 6.1 (--dump-ground), the level ground passage (the 8 m tile), and 7.2 (the build times, and why there's no per-plan cache).
