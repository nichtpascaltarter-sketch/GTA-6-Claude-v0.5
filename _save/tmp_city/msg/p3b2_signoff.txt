- For authors, worldc's summary now counts the interiors' plans: "library 3 looks, 3 shells (36 rooms, 30 inside walls, 35 doors, 3 stairs)". 3b-3 builds the interiors from this data.

HOW
- worldmodel now reads what the interiors need from the layouts: the doors' hinge and swing, the rooms' lux, and the stair records (from, to, run, w, kind, treads, rise, landing, walk).
- The compiler (worldmodel_buildings.cpp) adds to each shell:
  - each floor's rooms, in its layout's order: type, light schedule (as its windows get it), lux and polygon;
  - the inside walls, with their doors;
  - every door's leaf, exterior doors included: the end its hinges are at (toward the wall's a or b), the side it opens to, and the rooms each side of its wall, found from the room polygons (outside: none);
  - the stairs: each floor's layout's, with their ends resolved on the plan's floors (f1.hall, or relative 0:, +1: and -1:). treads and rise are derived from the floors as worldv derives them, and landing defaults to w.
- What a door's words mean (now in NTW_FORMAT 9.8):
  - swing=in opens into the building through an exterior wall, and into the room leads names second between two rooms; out is the other way;
  - hinge= is seen from the side the door is pushed from;
  - the defaults are in and left, and a mirrored placement swaps the sides.
- "library" v4 carries all this; buildingsbin.h documents the layout. The reader refuses:
  - a window in an inside wall;
  - an opening or stair naming a room its floor lacks, or a stair from or to a floor past the shell's;
  - a kind past its list.
  - Bounds: 88 bytes a shell, 44 a room (with its 3 points), 28 an inside wall, 44 a door, 60 a stair.
- Docs: NTW_FORMAT 9.8 (door leaves) and 9.10 (what the compiler reads), HANDBUILT_WORLD 6.1.
- Two changes from the plan, for you to OK:
  - The InteriorDefs and gInteriors move to 3b-3, which builds and installs them together with their geometry. Installing them now would stream empty interiors and add their doors' colliders and their rooms' ambient volumes before anything is drawn.
  - In 3b-3, Building::interior stays -1 on the hand-built records. game/ai.cpp skips buildings with an interior as door sources, which would undo 3b-1's real doors. The hand-built set maps placement to interior itself, and it doesn't go through planInteriors or pickBuilding (your note on the unguarded bs.facades[b.facade]).
