What it does:
1. roads.h: RoadEdge::halfWidthSide(side) and sidewalkSide(side), side -1 left and +1 right, looking fwd.
   - They read two new trims, how much narrower each side is than halfWidth and sidewalk. The trims are 0 unless set, so in the procedural world the accessors return halfWidth and sidewalk exactly.
2. roadoverrides.h:
   - roadKerbLine(laneWidth, median, lanesF, lanesB, kerb, side), the one formula for a side's kerb line:
     - two-way: laneWidth * that side's lanes + median / 2 + its kerb (proposal point 6);
     - one-way: laneWidth * (lanesF + lanesB) / 2 + median / 2 + its kerb, centred as lanes.cpp lays them out (amendment B).
   - EdgeExtra::parkingOn(side) (amendment A): the parking as written; where nothing is written (-1), the whole kerb strip if it is 1.8 m or more, else none. The AI can use it or keep its own copy of the rule.
3. The compile (worldmodel_roads.cpp): halfWidth is the wider side's kerb line.
   - For one-way edges and equal lane counts that is today's formula, to the bit: the pilot's world.bin is byte-identical with 65's worldc on the same files.
   - A two-way edge with unequal counts gets its wider side's (a 2/1 avenue with a 1 m right shoulder: 7.20, was 5.65).
4. roadsbin.h applyRoadSides(ov, roads) sets each edge's trims from EdgeExtra. Without overrides it changes nothing. Three callers:
   - handworld (the game in hand mode, right after reading the overrides);
   - loadRoadsFile (so traffic_sim's edges have their sides too);
   - worldc (so the blocks go on each side's sidewalk once step 2's queries read the sides).
5. Docs:
   - NTW_FORMAT: amendment A's parking rule, and each side's layout (two-way lanes out from the shape, one-way centred, then the kerb strip, then the sidewalk). RoadNetwork's width and sidewalk are the wider side's.
   - HANDBUILT_WORLD: the accessors.
6. Tests (testRoadOverrides):
   - on every edge of the model fixture: halfWidth is the wider side's kerb line, each side's kerb line and sidewalk after applyRoadSides, and the wider side exactly the network's;
   - nothing changes without overrides, and an edge without trims returns halfWidth and sidewalk to the bit;
   - the one-way lane cl.e0004: centred kerb lines (2.50 both sides), its sidewalk on the left only;
   - parkingOn: as written (2.40), unwritten on a street (its 2.40 m strip), unwritten on a lane (a 1.0 m strip: none), a shoulder= alone (none);
   - the 2/1 avenue: halfWidth 7.20 (the right side), left 3.10;
   - loadRoadsFile applies the sides: its trims equal applyRoadSides'.
