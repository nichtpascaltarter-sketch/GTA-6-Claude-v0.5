THE WAVY LINE (the side check)
- What draws it: the renderer's asphalt detail, your first guess.
  - src/shaders/world.hlsl, roadWear(), draws "longitudinal cracks meandering near the lane joints" on every road segment.
  - Along some stretches it seals them: "a wide glossy tar band over the crack instead of an open line". That band is about 9 cm wide (lineMask half-width 0.045 m), its albedo is halved and its roughness drops to 0.35.
  - At a low angle it catches the sky by day and the lamps by night. In the night shot above it reads dark.
- Why there:
  - The joints come every 3.5 m of the road's u, ±0.3 m of meander. roadmesh.cpp counts u in metres from the road surface's left edge.
  - Calle Siete's edges run east, so their left is the north kerb. The carriageway is 11.4 m: two 3.3 m lanes and two 2.4 m parking strips.
  - So the third joint, at u=10.5 m, lies 0.9 m inside the south kerb, in the parking strip.
- By design, in both modes and on every street. It's not a seam between mesh pieces and not a decal. It's the renderer's shader, not hand-road code I own, so I'm proposing no slice.
- A possible tweak for the shader's owner, if they want the cracks on the lane lines:
  - The shader only knows the carriageway's width, which travels in the colour alpha.
  - Placing the joints from the centre in 3.3 m steps would put them on the lane lines of these streets, at 2.4 m from each kerb and at the centre line, and on Avenida Flores' 2/2 lanes too.
