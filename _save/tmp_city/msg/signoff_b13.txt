CHANGELOG (player-facing)
Roofs from the air:
- Roof plant. Flat roofs of three floors and up now carry a row of plant along the back of the roof. That covers mid-rises, condos, offices, tower tops without a crown, and the walk-ups' and lofts' roofs. The row has:
  - the stair bulkhead;
  - the lift motor room on five floors and up;
  - one to four packaged HVAC units, by roof size;
  - on big roofs, a cooling tower with its fan stack.
  The small clutter (vents, dishes, units, solar panels) keeps to the rest of the roof. The masses show at every distance. Close up they get copings, a steel door, louvres, fans and rails. The bulkhead, motor room, cooling tower and tank have collision.
- Water tanks. Wooden tanks on braced steel stands, along the back of the roof, now stand on most of the older walk-ups in Calle Luna, North Porto Sol, Midtown and the Flats: 7 in 10 brick walk-ups, fewer of the others, some lofts. They show from afar. Before, a tank came by chance, sat anywhere on the roof and showed only up close.
- Copings. A parapet's cap is a shade lighter than its wall, so the roofs read with a light rim from the air.
- Balconies. The beach's and Key Coral's residential towers have a balcony slab with a glass railing at every floor, on the long faces or on all four faces of a near-square tower. The condo blocks have them on the front and back. From a distance they read as white bands.
- The towers' glass-lantern crowns now show from afar too. Past 420 m they used to drop out and leave a bare roof.
- Antenna masts and dishes on some of the taller mid-rise roofs (4 floors and up).
Ranch rows and villas:
- Ranch houses (Westbrook, the Grove) have their own palette: white, cream, butter, peach, coral, sage, mint, sky, grey-blue, warm grey, tan, lilac. About a sixth are brick. Trim is white, a deeper tone of the wall, or dark bronze. The rows used to be white on white. Two-storey and split-level houses take some of the ranch colours too.
- Ranch porches: a long low porch under a shed roof over the door on box ranches, and one in the inner corner on L ranches. 55% of the Grove's ranches have one, 38% of Westbrook's tract rows.
- Villas (Key Coral, the Grove, Bay Isles):
  - Mediterranean villas come plain (with the tiled door portico), with an arcaded loggia across the front and a terrace on it, or with a balcony on stone corbels over the entry.
  - Half the box-shaped colonial villas have a full-width veranda of round white columns with a balustraded balcony on top.
  - Modern villas: just over half have a roof terrace with a white pergola and a glass rail.
- Garage doors come in white, almond, wood brown or warm grey.
