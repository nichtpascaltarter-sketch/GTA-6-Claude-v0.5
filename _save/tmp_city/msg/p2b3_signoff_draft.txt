HOW
- Catalog: an optional piece field body=WxDxH, the solid part the collider takes where that isn't the whole piece; size= where it isn't written. The pilot catalog gives it to its 5 trees (0.52 m, the physics' trunk), the street light (0.34 m, the physics' pole) and the café umbrella (its 0.10 m pole; the physics has no umbrella collider). worldv checks that body= is WxDxH and fits inside size= (piece.body, with a bad fixture).
- "blocks.colliders": per collider its kind (box, cylinder), prop (an index into "blocks"' props), centre, bottom and top z, half extents and the box's width axis. A prop gets one when its piece has collide=box or cylinder: body= or else size=, times the prop's scale; a box turned with the prop; from the prop's base up. readBlocks refuses one naming a prop the blocks don't have.
- HandBlocks::colliderNear(p, margin, height), inline, by cell: siteColliderNear asks it first in hand mode. The procedural world has no hand blocks, so nothing changes there.
