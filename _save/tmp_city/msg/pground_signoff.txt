HOW
- worldmodel_blocks.cpp (worldc):
  - junctionCorners(): a level slab under each piece of every drawn corner sidewalk. It follows roadmesh.cpp's corner (the approaches' kerbs at their cut-backs, the curve through the kerb lines' meeting point, out by the narrower sidewalk), 5 cm in from its kerb and outer edge, 2 cm under its drawn top. There are six pieces on a curve and four on a straight one, each level at its lower end, so a sloping corner steps by a centimetre or two. The ground query takes the slabs as level ground (padHeight), and they are drawn out of sight under the sidewalk.
  - You asked for the terrain to be raised there. It can't be: its grid is 8 m, so raising it under a 2.5 m corner would lift the road's edge and the next sidewalk with it. The slabs are what the terrain would have been, only where the corner is.
  - A levelled parcel whose ground covers it now grades the terrain 0.3 m under its grade (BlockPad.covered, worldmodel.h), as the roads do. Otherwise the 8 m terrain between two parcels levelled at different heights rises through the lower one's ground.
  - groundAt (the height worldc puts records on) takes a levelled parcel's grade where one covers the point, since the terrain under it is now lower, and the roads' grading cuts it under the parcel's front anyway.
- The data, from my authoring script dress.py (scratch, as before; its output is the data):
  - It gives each parcel grade = (lowest sidewalk top along its outline) - 0.2. A sidewalk more than 0.1 m over the front street's highest does not count: Bougainvillea Boulevard's ramp past b001's west end.
  - It moves each vertex within 1.3 m of a street's edge 0.5 m in under it. A vertex at a junction, by two streets, goes to the two offset lines' meeting point, at the narrower sidewalk, since the corner is drawn that wide. A vertex of one parcel that lies on another's side is first added to that side, so that neighbours still meet when they move.
  - It cuts the promenade strips where the Paseo climbs more than 0.12 m, by its 4 m samples.
  - Only the generated parts of the 25 block files change: grade= on every parcel, the moved outlines, and b001's terraces. The dressing (trees, props, lights) is exactly as before.
  - h09-h12: z 3.50, 3.49, 3.47, 3.44 become 3.67, 3.65, 3.63, 3.61.
- wildlife.cpp findPerches: covered() now looks only at the boxes in its point's cell of a grid. The grid holds the boxes among the colliders near the anchor, over their extent, at most 48 by 48 cells of 4 m or more, built once per call. The test is the same, so the perches are the same.
