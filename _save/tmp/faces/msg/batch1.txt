What players see:
Eyes
- Real eyeballs. A clear cornea bulges over the iris. The iris has a dark pupil, a warm inner ring on light eyes, a lighter collarette, radial fibres and crypts, and a dark ring at its edge.
- Eye whites are no longer paper-white. They are yellower with age, a shade darker on very dark skin, and darker in the corners and under the upper lid, which makes the eyes less bug-eyed.
- Lids have thickness:
  - a dark lash-root edge on the upper lid, which reads like natural liner from a distance;
  - a moist pink waterline along the lower lid;
  - a pink caruncle at the inner corner.
  The lid margins no longer sink into the eyeball, and the crease and fold stop at the eye corners instead of running past them.
- Upper lashes come in two rows, longer and curled. Lower lashes are short and lighter.
- Brows are made of 70-130 individual hairs in the natural growth pattern: the head stands up, the body sweeps outwards, the tail runs out. They thin at the edges and sit over a faint tint of skin, instead of a painted band.
Skin
- Every skin vertex now carries data for the renderer's new skin shading:
  - the region it belongs to (lips, wet tissue, eyelids, ears, nose, nails, mouth);
  - how thin the tissue is, for translucency;
  - pore strength by face zone;
  - per person: oiliness, age band and melanin.
- Each person has their own oily T-zone of forehead, nose and chin.
- Skin colour gains:
  - more blush on the cheeks, nose and ears;
  - a tint on the upper lids;
  - a slightly darker ring round the mouth on medium and dark skin;
  - shaped lip colour (a fuller, lighter middle to the lower lip and a crisper border);
  - fine vertical lines on the lips.
- The brow ridge keeps sky light off the upper lids, so in flat or overhead light the eyes sit in the face instead of bulging.
Ears
- A deeper helix, antihelix and bowl on a finer mesh.
Beards and stubble
- Beards are no longer a dark mask. They are two or three layers of short hair cards rooted in the skin and combed the way beards grow: the mustache down and out, the chin down and a little forward, and back along the underside of the jaw. A few longer hairs stand off the edge for a soft outline.
- The solid beard mass is only the dense core now. Some skin shows through, and over the last centimetre it fades into the skin instead of ending in a hard edge.
- Beards reach the lips. There is no pale ring round the mouth, and no grey shelf under the lower lip.
- Stubble is a layer of short hairs over the shaved-shadow tint.
Hairline
- The hairline is soft. The hair fades into the skin over its first ~9 mm, with grain, and two staggered rows of fine short hairs fall onto the forehead. At the temples they thin out and run back towards the ear.
- The hairline's edge wanders smoothly instead of zig-zagging. Cards are narrower and sparser near the edge, and the ends of long hair vary smoothly instead of randomly per strip.
- The distant LOD keeps the hair's own colour at the hairline.
Budget
- The close-up mesh budget rises from 33.6k to 36k triangles to pay for the face's hair cards; the decimator takes the rest from flat body and clothing areas. The distant LODs (4.5k and 1.5k) are unchanged.
