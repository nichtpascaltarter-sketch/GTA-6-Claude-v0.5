CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Lips look like lips, not a coral lozenge. They were the skin's colour turned redder, so they kept its yellow and read
  salmon or coral at conversation distance; now they are a rose that suits each skin tone (rose-pink on fair skin,
  muted rose-brown on medium and deep skin), darker and redder than the skin round them and less yellow.
- Older people's lips are paler and closer to their skin, with a softer edge, as real lips get with age.
- The mouth corners are a little darker (they read as the corners of a mouth, not the ends of a painted shape).
- Everyone's lips change, the protagonists' included: Mari's and Dex's lips are rose now instead of coral. Shapes,
  lip sync and expressions are unchanged.

HOW (face.cpp: the vermilion's vertex colour)
- Measured as CIELAB of the vertex albedo (tools/lipstat.cpp): the lower lip's middle against the chin. Skin-colour
  measurements of real lips put the lower lip about 8-13 L* darker than the chin on light and medium skin, 8-10 a*
  redder and 5-7 b* less yellow (lip hue angle about 25-30 degrees). Main had the lightness and the redness about
  right, but b* -1.6 to +1.7: as yellow as the chin.
- Changes:
  - the vermilion's colour x (0.96, 1, 1.22) in linear RGB: less yellow (b* -5.3 to -7.3 against the chin), lightness
    and redness nearly unchanged;
  - with age the lips fade towards the skin round them (35% at 80) and the vermilion border softens (its transition
    widened by up to 0.4 degrees of the head grid at 80). A softened border's skin-side row takes a little of the lip
    colour but stays skin (its region, gloss and shading as before);
  - the corners' darkening 18% -> 25%.
- Tuning: three variants in game (main, x(0.96,1,1.22), x(0.93,1,1.32)) after host previews; the first rose won
  (the stronger one drifts towards mauve on fair men).
