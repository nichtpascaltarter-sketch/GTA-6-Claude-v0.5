CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Lips look like lips, not a coral lozenge. They were the skin's colour made redder, so they kept its yellow and read
  salmon or coral at conversation distance. Now they are a rose that suits each skin tone: rose-pink on fair skin,
  muted rose-brown on medium and deep skin. They are darker and redder than the skin round them, and less yellow.
- Older people's lips are paler and closer to their skin, with a softer edge, as real lips get with age.
- The corners of the mouth are a little darker, so they read as the corners of a mouth, not the ends of a painted
  shape.
- Everyone's lips change, the protagonists' included: Mari's and Dex's lips are rose now instead of coral. Shapes,
  lip sync and expressions are unchanged.

HOW (face.cpp: the vermilion's vertex colour)
- Measured as CIELAB of the vertex albedo (my host tool lipstat.cpp): the middle of the lower lip against the chin.
  Skin-colour measurements of real lips put the lower lip about 8-13 L* darker than the chin on light and medium
  skin, 8-10 a* redder and 5-7 b* less yellow (a lip hue angle of about 25-30 degrees). Main had the lightness and
  the redness about right, but its b* was -1.6 to +1.7 against the chin: as yellow as the chin.
- Changes:
  - the vermilion's colour x (0.96, 1, 1.22) in linear RGB. It is now 5.3-7.3 b* less yellow than the chin, with
    lightness and redness nearly unchanged;
  - with age the lips fade towards the skin round them (35% at age 1.0, the oldest);
  - with age the vermilion border softens: its transition widens from 0.6 to up to 1.4 degrees of the head grid. A
    softened border's skin-side row takes a little of the lip colour but stays skin (its region, gloss and shading as
    before);
  - the corners' darkening goes from 18% to 25%.
- Tuning: three variants in game (main, x(0.96,1,1.22), x(0.93,1,1.32)) after host previews. The first rose won;
  the stronger one drifts towards mauve on fair men.
