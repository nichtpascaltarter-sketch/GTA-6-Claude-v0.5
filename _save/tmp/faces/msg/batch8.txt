CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Buzz cuts look like short hair now, not a smooth, shiny swim cap. Up close you see a fine grain, a soft outline and
  a soft hairline; under a street lamp the top has a fine sprinkle of highlights instead of a satin band.
- Faded buzz cuts get shorter hairs down the clippered sides and none where they are taken to the skin.
- Nothing else changes: the other hairstyles, hair under hats, the protagonists (Mari wears a bun, Dex a short cut)
  and the crowd LODs (from 15 m buzz cuts look as before).

HOW
- Buzz cuts (8 of the viewer's first 80 people) had no strand cards: only the 2.5 mm shell, a smooth cap with the
  shell's sheen. They now get scalp cards like the other styles, tuned for short hair:
  - many small one-segment cards (4-6 mm long, 7 mm wide, 8 mm apart, density 0.5). With one segment each, twice
    as many fit the scalp cards' existing budget, so the grain is fine;
  - each card's direction scattered about the crown-whorl flow: up close the highlights sprinkle instead of lining
    up into a band, or leaving dark patches outside it;
  - deeper in the hair volume (depth 5 / 3) and with freshly cut tips (no sun-lightening): a sprinkle over the
    shell's soft sheen, not a bright frost;
  - the hairline's fine hairs at half length and 0.6x density (depth 4). In the first prototype the front edge read
    as a white frosted band in the sun and under the lamp;
  - a fade: hairs shorten down the clippered sides (0.5-1x with the fade) and stop where it is taken to the skin.
- Tuning: the first prototype and four variants were shot in game (people 6, 27, 35 at 0.5 and 1 m, by day and at
  night). v1 (depth, colours, fringe) removed the frost; v4 (scattered flow) broke up the dark patches at night;
  v7 (small one-segment cards + scattered flow) gave the finest grain with neither problem. v7 ships.
  Dropped: tinting the shell towards the scalp (v2: no visible change).
