HOW (face.cpp)
- Ear (addEar). Measured with a new host tool that casts sight lines into the ear against the character's triangles,
  culling back faces as skin is drawn:
  - within 3.5 mm of the canal's centre, 24-35% of the sight lines from the side met the head's skin before the ear,
    on every person (from 40 degrees behind: all of them);
  - the canal's innermost ring sat 8.4 mm inside the head on Dex (the relief takes the canal about 24 mm in along
    the ear's axis);
  - the canal's cap stood 12-13 mm up off the canal's floor in a spike. Its depth predates the relief's x1.75 scale.
  Fix:
  - the canal and the concha's floor (the front rings inside 0.45 of the ear's radius) are pushed out along the
    ear's axis until they clear the head's surface by 1.5 mm, and the cap sits at the canal's floor;
  - the canal's opening is painted darker (x0.35 at the canal, fading out by a quarter of the radius; the cap x0.2
    instead of x0.45), so the now shallower canal still reads as a hole.
- Lips (lipBlueLift). On fair skin, the blue lift (x1.22 from batch 10) now stops where the lip colour's CIELAB hue
  angle comes down to 28 degrees. Real lips are about 25-30 degrees.
  - Pink-toned fair skin has little yellow to take out: the full lift took Dex's lower lip to 24 degrees (lavender).
  - The floor fades in over skin luminance 0.15-0.3, so deep skin keeps the full lift and its deeper, cooler lips.
    Without that fade, deep-skinned men such as 13 and 57 (24-27 degrees) would have changed too.
  - Your L*-scaled 1.22 -> 1.12, tested on the host: it moves every light skin (17 from 38 to 43 degrees; db*
    against the chin up to -2.1) and fails LipColour on 2 of its 12 faces. The hue floor changes only the people
    at risk: of the 15 people measured, only Mari and Dex.
