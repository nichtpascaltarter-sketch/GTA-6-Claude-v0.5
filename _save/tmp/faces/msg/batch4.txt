What players see:
Brows
- The skin under each brow now carries the brow's colour across the brow's whole body, so brows read as dark shapes instead of a few loose hairs, most at a few metres. Before, that tint sat a third of the brow's height above the hairs and between two rows of the face mesh 4 mm apart, so most brows got almost none of it. anim_test now checks it: on main, 2 of 10 test faces had their darkest brow skin at 0.88 and 0.96 of the forehead; now every face is at 0.54 or below.
- Two more rows of the face mesh run through the brow (about 2 mm apart there), so the tint follows the brow's shape, thin brows included.
Hairlines and beard edges
- Hair and beard shells now rise out of the skin at their edge instead of ending in a 1.2 mm rolled lip. Where they are still thin they shade with the skin's own normal, not their thickness ramp's, which tilted them about 20 degrees.
- The buzz cut's hairline loses its light rim and the dark line under it (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-GTA-6-Claude-v0-5/cc7ce613-fe8a-5fa0-8160-c9078e8be173/scratchpad/faces/hairline_ab0_ab1_d8a.png).
- The bearded men's night cheek line is much fainter on c27 and fainter on c38 (b4_1m_night_beards.png and b4_1m_night_highpass.png in the same folder). What is left is the hair shading's wrap on the beard shell against the skin's terminator. That fix is renderer-side; see my earlier message.
Curls
- Curly coils are rounder: five sides and six stations per turn, instead of four sides and three stations, which twisted into flat ribbons.
