What players see:
Beards and stubble
- A beard's back edge now follows the jaw: from the sideburn in front of the ear it runs back round the angle of the jaw, instead of stopping in a vertical cut in front of it. In profile a full beard no longer reads as a block stuck on the face.
- The cheek line and the back edge wander a few millimetres (smooth noise), and under the jaw the beard thins out over the last rows above the neck instead of ending in a shelf.
- Stubble and the shaved shadow follow the same outline, so Dex's new stubble wraps his jaw.
Hairlines
- The hairline curve runs smoothly through its control points instead of in straight segments, which put kinks into it (a notch at the temple in profile).
- Women's sideburns are short curves instead of a man's V-shaped sideburn in front of the ear; men keep theirs, varying a little per person.
- Hair over the ears (long hair, bobs, women's curls) now drops to the ear smoothly from the temple; with the softer sideburn it would otherwise have stepped, a horizontal line beside the eyes.
Age
- The temples hollow behind the eye socket's rim with age (more on lean faces, little on heavy ones).
Skin and lips
- Each person gets their own undertone at the same depth of skin: rosier on some, more olive or golden on others, golden more often on East Asian faces. The hue shifts a few percent at constant brightness.
- The lower lip's border blends softly into the skin below it: the tight blend left a groove all round the lip that the baked shading drew as a dark outline.
