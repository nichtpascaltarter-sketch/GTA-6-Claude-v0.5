What players see:
Eyes ("goggles" gone)
- The lids now flow into the eye socket. Before, the lid mound met the socket in a 3-4 mm groove that ringed each eye: the baked shade made it a dark ring, and the lid rows' fans at the corners folded into pleats that streaked out to the temples. Now:
  - the lids blend softly into the socket;
  - the inner corner is filled, so the nose's side sweeps into it;
  - the shading round the eyes is relaxed;
  - the upper lid's crease is carried by the lid rows themselves.
- Dark skin round the eyes stays only under the eye and at the inner corner, instead of a ring all round.
- The lower waterline is darker, as it sits in the lashes' shade, so there are no bright rims round the eyes.
- Men get a lighter lash line.
- Eye whites are a shade darker on very dark skin.
- Together with the renderer's feature shadows on main, the eyes now sit in the socket's shade at night instead of glowing.
Brows and brow ridge
- Brows sit about 2.5 mm higher, leaving a strip of brow-bone skin above the lid's fold, so brow, socket and lashes no longer merge into one dark bar at 4 m.
- The head of the brow, by the nose, is sparser and the tail thinner.
- The skin tint under the brow body is a little stronger, so the brow still reads as a soft shape where the hairs thin out on screen.
- The brow ridge stands a few millimetres prouder of the eyes. The cornea now sits on average 6.4 mm behind the brow front (it was 5.3), less for women. The renderer agreed this is enough for its brow shadow.
Hair beside the eyes
- Batch 1's dark and pale stripes at the temples are gone (worst on c17's curls). The soft, skin-tinted hair edge is now limited to the front hairline. At the temples and sides the hair keeps a solid, hair-coloured edge: curtains, coils and sideburns hang over the face's edge there, and the faded shell showed through between them as stripes.
- Beards: a thinner rim where the beard meets the skin, and a softer edge colour.
Lips, ears, skin
- Lips have a satin sheen instead of lacquer (gloss 0.85 -> 0.65; the renderer agreed). Men's lips are a shade less red.
- Ears are less red.
- Older skin is a little blotchier.
- Forehead lines no longer box in the frown lines.
Speed
- Brows are built about twice as fast. Building a character's full mesh is a few percent faster than batch 1 (anim_test side by side: 141->133 and 150->141 ms).
