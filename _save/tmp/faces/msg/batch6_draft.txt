CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Each eye socket is now closed at the side by the lateral orbital rim: the cheekbone's frontal process rising to the brow's tail. Before, the socket's carve left a trench 6-10 mm deep at eye level, running past the outer eye corner to the temple. Its upper wall faced steeply down, so under a lamp or a high sun the socket's shade ran on to the side of the face. Now the skin beside the eye runs smoothly from the brow's tail to the cheekbone; the trench is at most 2.5 mm.
- The pale crescent under each eye is gone. The lower lid's skin edge had taken the margin strip's normal (facing straight up, +68 deg), so the top of the lower lid lit up as a white "smile" under the iris. It now keeps a rounded edge (+24 deg), and only the 1 mm margin itself catches the light.
- Honest size of the effect: it shows best at 0.5 m by day (the lower lids) and from three-quarter views under a lamp (no dark patch beside the outer eye corner). Straight on at 1 and 4 m it is subtle. The night "goggles" at 4 m stay: the sockets get almost no light at night (see the renderer note below).
