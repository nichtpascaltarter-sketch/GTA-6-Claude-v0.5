CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Brows look like hair again, not solid black pencil strokes.
  - Why: since snapshot 36, overlapping strand cards add up, and about nine overlap through a brow's body, so the dark brows of Mari, c17, c27 and c38 had turned solid at 0.5 m.
  - Fix: brow cards are now 0.45x as dense. The body still reads full, a little skin shows between the hairs, and the edges are soft.
- Lashes look like a fringe again, not eyeliner. Lash cards are at 0.75x density:
  - top row 0.95 -> 0.71;
  - second row 0.32-0.55 -> 0.24-0.41;
  - lower lid 0.32 -> 0.24.
- Final values differ from the plan I sent (cards 0.55x, tint 0.5x, lashes 0.7x). I shot four variants in game under snapshot 36's shaders:
  - k4: cards 0.38x, tint 0.5x;
  - k6: cards 0.55x, tint 0.85x;
  - k7: cards 0.45x, tint 1x;
  - the planned mix.
  - Halving the skin tint made the brows faint at 1 m again: c17 dropped back to 0.6 stops below her forehead. So the tint stays, and the cards' density alone carries the fix (k7).
