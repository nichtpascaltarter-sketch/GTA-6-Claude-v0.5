CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- At conversation distance the eyes no longer sit in a dark "goggle" bar. The brows, the shaded lids and the eyes
  used to read as one horizontal band across the face, with a hard top edge under the brows and a dark bridge
  between the eyes, most of all under street lamps. Now each eye sits in its own softly shaded socket, the lids
  catch light under the brow, and the bridge of the nose between the eyes stays lit.
- Everyone's face changes a little, the protagonists included: the brow-to-lid transition is smoother, and the
  root of the nose between the brows is fuller (a shallower notch in profile). Faces stay recognisable.

HOW (face.cpp, the face's signed-distance model)
- The cause, measured on the SDF profile (tools: profiles through the eye centre, the midline and half way):
  - under the brow, the upper lid's dome met the brow ridge in a ledge: the surface stepped 2-6.4 mm forward per mm
    of height (one face 6.4), 4-7 mm in all. Under a lamp or a high sun: a lit dome under a hard shadow line;
  - between the brows, the midline sank into a groove 2-6.3 mm behind the brows' heads (the nasion carve, 19 mm wide,
    cut into the glabella): that groove stays in the brow ridge's shade, the dark bridge joining both sockets.
- Fixes:
  - a soft fill under the brow ridge, over the lid's fold (an ellipsoid with radii 13 x 5.25 x 2.5 mm, 6 mm blend):
    the face now rises from the lid to the brow as a slope;
  - the nasion carve sits 7 mm further forward (shallower), and a small fill keeps the glabella over the nasion level
    with the brows' heads (radii 6.5 x 6 x 7.5 mm, 6 mm blend). The nasion is still the notch at the root of the nose:
    3.3-7.8 mm behind the glabella over six faces (Mari 5.4, Dex 6.4), instead of 7.1-12.9 mm (Mari 10.1, Dex 11.5).
- Tuning in game (dev knobs, removed): lid fill alone lightened the lids 0.2-0.7 stops but left the bridge dark; the
  nasion carve alone barely moved it (-0.1..-0.3 stops); the glabella fill closes the groove.
