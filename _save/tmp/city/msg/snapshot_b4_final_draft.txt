CHANGELOG (player-facing)
- Two new house types. Fifties Florida block houses (CBS) have pastel stucco, a low hip roof in white, tile or white metal, aluminium window awnings (plain or striped) on most, and a carport. They stand among the bungalows of North Porto Sol, Calle Luna, the Flats and Midtown, and in Westbrook, the small towns and the Keys. Folk Victorians are two-storey frame houses with a cross gable, a porch with brackets in the inner corner, a king-post truss and an attic window in the front gable, and a two-strip driveway. They are in Okahatchee, Harlow, Fort Castell and Key Solano, and among the farmhouses.
- Raised Keys houses in Key Solano, Ten Palms and the Keys. The living floor is a full storey up on concrete piers (the flood code). Parking is open underneath with storage closed in behind. Each has a front deck with a stair, a metal roof and storm shutters.
- Carports replace the garage wing on block houses and MiMo houses: a flat roof on steel posts with a utility room at the back. Half of the MiMo carports also get a screen of fins.
- More house shapes:
  - ranch and block houses with a gable-fronted bay forward of the front (the entry beside it);
  - L-shaped bungalows with the porch in the corner;
  - fewer plain villas on Key Coral and the bay islands.
- Gas stations stop being one design. There are four:
  - a flat canopy with a lit brand band;
  - a sixties butterfly canopy on tapered columns over a white kiosk with a coloured frame;
  - an old service station with a barrel-tile hip roof over a cream stucco kiosk;
  - a slab carried out from the kiosk.
  All get pump islands, and most a price sign on a pole at the street corner.
- Roofs: tile in three blends (terracotta, brown, aged dark red) and white roofs on some ranch houses. Westbrook no longer reads as one orange sheet from the air.
- Yard trees are placed in the front and back yards measured from the house. They no longer stand inside houses, porches, decks, stairs, pools, garages, carports, driveways or back-yard buildings: a blocked tree moves to the mirrored spot or the other yard. Before, 5,357 of 47,580 yard trees stood inside a building.
- Strip-mall and gas-station forecourts follow the ground. Before, a slab floated 0.35 m above it, and cars and shoppers sank into it. A 1 m walk with a curb runs along the shopfronts, walkable up to 0.5 m. Canopy supports, gas columns and pumps stand on the asphalt. Where the building is lifted to an embankment road's level, the forecourt is a solid plateau walled down to the ground (9 of 834).
