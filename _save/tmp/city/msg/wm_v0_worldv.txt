COMPILE RULES (now in NTW_FORMAT 9.4)
- Widths are as written, else the class's.
- A one-sided compound leaves the other side at 0.
- RoadNetwork's width is the same both sides (the wider side). The per-side values stay on Edge.
- control= overrides the derived control: none and yield are 0 (priority by road class), stop is 1 where every approach stops, else 0, and signal is 2. Without it, generate()'s rule applies.
  - The reason: the lane graph treats 1 as an all-way stop, so a yield, or a two-way stop, runs as priority until it reads the node's yield and stops lists. Your step-3 signal and yield checks will see the lane graph's view.
- Seeds hash the edge's id.
- Flags: RF_BRIDGE for a deck, RF_ONEWAY when a direction has no lanes, RF_UNPAVED for dirt, RF_NOSIDEWALK.
- An edge with an error is left out (RoadIndex::roadEdge -1), and the rest compiles.
