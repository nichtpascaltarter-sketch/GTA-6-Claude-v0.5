city: a plan for the Calle Luna pilot (stage 1, item 3), for your review. I'll start on part 1 once the roads sign-off is in, unless you say otherwise.

Scope: everything within 400 m of (1560, 350).
- The baked terrain there is flat: ground 2-4 m, mean 3.5.
- A river crosses the south of the circle and takes 13% of it.
- The Sol Expressway clips the north.
- Of the three places, mariApt (1771, 357) and the diner (1350, 343) stand on one E-W street, and the boatyard (1650, 239) is by the river.
- pressure stays inside the circle. low_tide (vargasGarage) and collateral (stashHouse) leave it, about 1 km out.

1. Streets (about a week)
  a. The pilot's roads, designed by hand. Today's grid serves for orientation only (D3), and nothing is exported from it. The files: world/world.ntw, world/districts/cl/district.ntw and roads.ntw, plus signal plans in lib/signals.
     - Calle Luna is the main street, with shops and flats over them. The diner and Mari's flat keep their spots on it.
     - A landscaped median boulevard.
     - Residential side streets, with alleys behind the shops for the back garages.
     - A curving river drive, with the boatyard on the water.
     - A bridge to the south bank.
     - Signals at the main crossings.
     - That comes to about 50 nodes and 80 edges. The expressway is trunk, so I'd leave it out of the pilot.
  b. worldc grades the terrain under the roads (3.3): the carriageway, kerbs and sidewalks, blended out over a few metres. It reuses today's flattenAlong and lowerAlong, moved out as roadnet.cpp was, and stores a delta on the bake. Without this, the hand roads float or sink.
  c. generateCell for hand mode (my role in 11.1) builds the road cells: the surface, markings, kerbs, sidewalks and collision, with no procedural buildings, lots or sites.
  d. A top-down check map: a scratch tool of mine for now, PNG, with the terrain, the hand roads at their widths and today's network as a faint underlay. The preview tools proper are the tools engineer's (11.1).
2. Blocks: the block compiler (parcels, lots, paths, dressing, lights), then the pilot's 25 or so blocks.
3. Library and buildings: the library compiler (plans, layouts, units), the first 240 or so items, the 439 placements, and the interiors with the interiors and AI agents.
4. places.ntw for the three places (the missions agent), then Act 1 in hand mode.
