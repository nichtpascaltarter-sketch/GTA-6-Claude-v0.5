WHAT
- The model, src/world/worldmodel.h (host tools only; worldv reviewed the header and its points are in):
  - discover(), loadParsed() and load();
  - typed records with file and line: districts and zones, nodes, edges with every record in their blocks, junctions, bus stops, signal plans, transit, places and anchors, blocks with parcels and lots, buildings' headers and placement, library items;
  - references resolved by the schema's table, with the same rules as worldv's ref checks;
  - findings with stable codes.
- compileRoads() turns the model into today's RoadNetwork:
  - widths as written, else the class's;
  - heights from the profiles or the node z (no terrain read);
  - seeds from the edge ids;
  - junction radii, cut-backs and control as generate() derives them, with control= overriding;
  - the spatial hash;
  - plus a RoadIndex back to the ids and lines.
- roads.cpp: its network core moves to roadnet.cpp, which roads.cpp includes, so host tools can link the core without the generator's world. The core is the class table, positions along an edge, the hash and its queries, and the junction pass, which becomes RoadNetwork::finishJunctions(). The move is mechanical: 194 lines, plus the include and the one call. The procedural world is unchanged. rfcheck's census covers every edge, node, building, bus stop and route, and the site and story places: 55,085 record lines, all identical to the road fix's. Only its 30 timing lines differ.
- ntwschema: the reference patterns are parsed and matched in one place (parseRefPattern, matchRefs, refRules). It is worldv's matcher moved over, so worldv can drop its copy.
- NTW_FORMAT 9.4 gains what the roads compiler requires: shape ends on its nodes; profiles from 0 to the shape's length at the nodes' z; one-sided kerb compounds leave the other side bare; the symmetric widths; seeds from ids; how yield and two-way stops run until the lane graph reads the lists.
- world.bin is unchanged: worldc doesn't use the model until stage 1, and world/ has no roads yet.
