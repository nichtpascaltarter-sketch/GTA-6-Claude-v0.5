- Gas stations stop being one design: a flat canopy with a lit brand band, a sixties butterfly canopy on tapered columns over a white kiosk with a coloured frame, an old service station with a barrel-tile hip roof over a cream stucco kiosk, or a slab carried out from the kiosk. They also get pump islands and, on most, a price sign on a pole at the street corner.
- Raised Keys houses in Key Solano, Ten Palms and the Keys: the living floor on concrete piers a full storey up (the flood code), parking open underneath and storage closed in behind, a front deck with a stair, a metal roof and storm shutters.
- Suburban roofs: stronger tile blends (terracotta, brown, aged red) and some white roofs on the ranch houses, so Westbrook reads less orange from the air.
