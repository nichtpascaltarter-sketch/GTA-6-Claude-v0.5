WHAT IT DOES
The lower road gives way where its carriageway lies on top of a bigger road's.
- Who gives way: rank highway > boulevard > avenue > rural > street > lane > dirt. A bridge beats the rest of its class;
  then the longer road by name.
- What counts: a stretch of the lower road beside the bigger one at under 32 degrees, with its centreline on the bigger
  road's asphalt for 8 m or more outside both junction areas. This takes in all that the AI's --overlaps counts.
- The whole overlapping run goes. The rest of the edge goes too when under 20 m would be left, or under 40 m running on
  beside the bigger road.
- Cut ends are joined back at about 65 degrees, at a new junction.
- Tidying:

    junction-pair merge: Sol Beach 2544 into 2546, Venetia 2561 into 2559, Midtown 281/283 gone with the stub. Otherwise
