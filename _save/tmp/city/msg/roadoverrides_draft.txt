WHERE IT FITS
- The hand-built roads compile to today's RoadNetwork. That's the world model's compileRoads, in snapshot 61.
- worldc will write RoadOverrides beside it into world.bin ("roads.overrides"), and the game will read it back in hand mode (gRoadOverrides).
- The procedural world passes nothing: an empty RoadOverrides, or nullptr, means today's behaviour exactly.
- Every list overrides a derivation only where something is authored, or adds what nothing can derive (NTW_FORMAT 9.4, HANDBUILT_WORLD 12).
- Indices are the RoadNetwork's. Distances are metres along the edge from n0. dir 0 is fwd (n0 to n1). Sides are seen looking fwd. Lanes count from the left of their direction of travel.

WHAT'S IN IT
- Per edge:
  - LaneCount: pockets, merges, drops, with add/drop side and taper;
  - LaneUse: bus, streetcar or bike lanes;
  - NoSwitch: solid lines;
  - Moves: the turns each arriving lane may make, as masks;
  - Crossing: signal, zebra, plain or unmarked, with width;
  - BusStop: id, side, kind, wait area, streetcar clearance;
  - Kerb stretches: parkbay, nostop, loading, taxirank, nojaywalk;
  - CurbCut;
  - Sign;
  - EdgeExtra, one per edge: speed, lane width, median and its barrier, the per-side kerb and sidewalk widths that RoadNetwork's symmetric halfWidth/sidewalk can't hold, and tunnel.
- Per node:
  - NodeRule: control as written (auto, none, stop, yield, signal), plan index, offset, compass approaches, stops (all or a list), yields, bulb radius;
  - Junction: kind (plain or roundabout), turns (all, no_uturn, custom), box, stop lines, connects (lane to lane, with turn and protected), bans, heads.
- Signal plans: cycle, legs and phases, with moves as written (n_lp) and walk lists.

ALREADY IN ROADNETWORK, FOR REFERENCE
compileRoads maps control= onto RoadNode::control in the meantime:
- none and yield → 0;
- stop → 1 where every approach stops, else 0;
- signal → 2.
The yield and stop lists, plans and approaches are only here.
