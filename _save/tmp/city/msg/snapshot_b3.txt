PROPOSAL (renderer; I can't change shaders): at tour stop 3 the restyled tower's storefront reads darker than on the old main. The facade shader multiplies the shop interior by the facade's glass tint (facade.hlsli ~line 359: glassTint = glassC), and a tower has one glass colour for its curtain wall and its lobby. As a stopgap, blockstyle.cpp lightens the tint 45% toward clear on storefront towers. A one-line fix in the shader would be: float3 glassTint = storefront ? lerp(glassC, float3(0.86, 0.9, 0.92), 0.6) : glassC; - the stopgap can then go.
