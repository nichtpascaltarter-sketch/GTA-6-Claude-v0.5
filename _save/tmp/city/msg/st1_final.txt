WHAT
- sites.cpp/.h: gTerrainBakeLandforms, false in the game. When true, SiteSet::layout runs only the two layouts that change land and water outside pads: Port Isle's quay walls, dredged ring and berth, and the Key Coral golf pond. It drops everything they place and skips the airfield, the landmarks, the places and the transit corridor, with their pads and grading. With the flag off, the code path is unchanged.
- gridcodec.cpp/.h: a lossless grid codec. It predicts each value from its neighbours (JPEG-LS's median predictor), Golomb-Rice codes the residuals with a parameter that adapts per local-activity context, and codes flat stretches as runs. The stream is bit-exact and portable, and each grid carries a 32-byte header with a hash. It packs the heights to 17.6% of raw (2.30 MB), against 3.44 MB for gzip -9 on the same row-delta data in the prototype.
- tools/terrainbake.cpp: a host tool that runs the generator once with the flag, without roads, and writes world/terrain/:
  - heights in cm (-44.93..166.85 m);
  - the water class with its levels (sea 0 m, lake 4 m);
  - today's region map;
  - the 640² coast distance at 0.25 m;
  - terrain.ntw, with the grid, the layers and their hashes, the water levels, the coast, island and lake outlines (88 islands) and the 7 channels.
  Every grid is decoded from disk and compared before the tool reports success.
- terrain.ntw is the first NTW file (the format of docs/HANDBUILT_WORLD.md 4.2). The runtime loader, worldc and embedding come in step 2.
